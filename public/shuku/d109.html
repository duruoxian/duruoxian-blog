长难句精析 30 句

全部为考研风格自编句。用法：先自己划主干→看拆解→口头翻译→次日回看。分六类各5句。

一、定语从句（含嵌套）

S1. The scientists who claimed that data collected from samples that were not randomly selected could be misleading have published a new study. - 拆解：主干 The scientists ... have published a new study；who claimed that... 修饰scientists；that data ... misleading 是claimed的宾语从句；collected from... 过去分词修饰data；that were not randomly selected 修饰samples。 - 译：那些声称"非随机抽样所得数据可能有误导性"的科学家发表了一项新研究。

S2. A society whose members regard education as a privilege rather than a right is unlikely to invest adequately in its schools. - 拆解：主干 A society is unlikely to invest...；whose members regard A as B 修饰society。 - 译：其成员把教育视为特权而非权利的社会，不太可能对学校充分投入。

S3. Companies that fail to adapt to the changes which digital technology has brought about will gradually lose the competitiveness on which their survival depends. - 拆解：主干 Companies will lose the competitiveness；that fail... 修饰companies；which digital technology has brought about 修饰changes（介词about挂在on which上）；on which their survival depends 修饰competitiveness。 - 译：未能适应数字技术所带来的变化的公司，将逐渐失去其生存所依赖的竞争力。

S4. The theory, which was once dismissed by scholars whose methods were considered beyond reproach, has regained credibility. - 拆解：主干 The theory has regained credibility；非限制性which从句插入；whose methods... 修饰scholars。 - 译：这一曾被某些学者否定的理论重新获得了认可——而这些学者的方法在当时被认为无可挑剔。

S5. There are times when the ability to tolerate uncertainty matters more than the knowledge we have accumulated. - 拆解：主干 There are times；when... 修饰times；to tolerate 不定式修饰ability；(that) we have accumulated 修饰knowledge（省略that）。 - 译：有时，容忍不确定性的能力比我们积累的知识更重要。

二、状语从句与让步

S6. Although the experiment, however carefully conducted, could not eliminate every source of error, its results were consistent enough to support the hypothesis. - 拆解：Although让步从句内插 however carefully conducted（省略it was）；主干 its results were consistent enough to...。 - 译：尽管实验再仔细也无法消除所有误差来源，其结果的一致性已足以支持该假设。

S7. Wherever regulations lag behind innovation, the gap tends to be filled by practices that are profitable rather than responsible. - 拆解：Wherever地点状语从句；主干 the gap tends to be filled by...；that are... 修饰practices。 - 译：凡是监管落后于创新之处，这一空白往往由"逐利而非负责"的做法填补。

S8. So long as the incentives remain unchanged, no amount of moral persuasion will alter corporate behavior. - 拆解：So long as条件从句；主干 no amount of... will alter...（否定+名词短语作主语）。 - 译：只要激励机制不变，再多的道德劝说也改变不了企业行为。

S9. While it is true that remote work offers flexibility, it does not follow that productivity will rise automatically. - 拆解：While让步 + it is true that...（形式主语）；主干 it does not follow that...（"并不意味着"）。 - 译：远程办公确实提供了灵活性，但这并不意味着生产力会自动提升。

S10. Provided that the data are interpreted with caution, they can still shed light on questions that direct observation cannot answer. - 拆解：Provided that条件；主干 they can shed light on...；that direct observation cannot answer 修饰questions。 - 译：只要解释时保持谨慎，这些数据仍能揭示直接观察无法回答的问题。

三、名词性从句与形式主语

S11. What distinguishes genuine science from pseudoscience is not the certainty of its conclusions but the willingness of its practitioners to abandon them when the evidence changes. - 拆解：What... 主语从句；主干 is not A but B；when the evidence changes 修饰abandon。 - 译：区分真科学与伪科学的，不是结论的确定性，而是从业者随证据改变而放弃结论的意愿。

S12. It is widely assumed that automation will free workers from drudgery, yet whether it does so depends on how the gains are distributed. - 拆解：It is assumed that...（形式主语）；yet 并列；whether... depends on how...（主语从句+宾语从句嵌套）。 - 译：人们普遍认为自动化会把劳动者从苦役中解放出来，但它是否真能如此，取决于成果如何分配。

S13. That the company survived the crisis owed much to decisions made long before the downturn became visible. - 拆解：That... 主语从句；主干 owed much to...；made long before... 修饰decisions（before从句后置）。 - 译：该公司之所以渡过危机，很大程度上要归功于衰退显现之前很久就做出的决策。

S14. Whether a regulation achieves its purpose is determined less by the letter of the law than by the rigor of its enforcement. - 拆解：Whether主语从句；主干 is determined less by A than by B（"与其说…不如说"）。 - 译：一项法规能否达成目的，与其说取决于法律条文，不如说取决于执行的严格程度。

S15. The committee failed to reach consensus on the question of whether the funds should be allocated according to need or to merit. - 拆解：主干 failed to reach consensus；whether...or... 介词of的宾语从句（同位语性质）。 - 译：委员会未能就"资金应按需求还是按绩效分配"达成一致。

四、倒装、强调与省略

S16. Not until the consequences became irreversible did the public begin to grasp the magnitude of the problem. - 拆解：Not until... 置句首 → 部分倒装 did the public begin...；主干 the public began to grasp... - 译：直到后果不可逆转，公众才开始意识到问题的严重性。

S17. Rarely does a single policy, however well designed, solve a problem whose roots lie in deep social structures. - 拆解：Rarely否定副词提前→倒装 does a policy solve...；however well designed 插入让步；whose roots... 修饰problem。 - 译：一项政策无论设计得多好，都很难解决根源深植于社会结构的问题。

S18. Only when the cost of inaction exceeds the cost of reform will vested interests accept change. - 拆解：Only when... 提前→倒装 will vested interests accept...；主干 vested interests accept change。 - 译：只有当不作为的代价超过改革代价时，既得利益者才会接受变革。

S19. It is not the absence of talent but the absence of patience that explains why so many promising projects collapse halfway. - 拆解：It is A but B that...（强调句）；why...宾语从句。 - 译：解释了这么多有前景的项目为何半途而废的，不是缺乏天赋，而是缺乏耐心。

S20. Vegetation, once destroyed, does not recover on its own, nor does the trust a community places in its institutions. - 拆解：once destroyed = once (it is) destroyed 省略；nor... 倒装 does the trust recover；a community places... 省略that的定语从句修饰trust。 - 译：植被一旦被毁便无法自愈，社区对自身制度的信任同样如此。

五、分词与独立主格

S21. Having been ignored for decades, the warnings of early researchers now seem prescient rather than pessimistic. - 拆解：Having been ignored 完成被动分词作状语（先发生）；主干 the warnings seem...。 - 译：早期研究者的警告被忽视了几十年，如今看来是先见之明而非悲观。

S22. With the population aging and the workforce shrinking, the pension system designed half a century ago is under increasing strain. - 拆解：With+名词+doing 独立主格作原因状语；主干 the system is under strain；designed... 修饰system。 - 译：随着人口老龄化与劳动力萎缩，半个世纪前设计的养老金体系承受着越来越大的压力。

S23. The report, based on interviews conducted over three years, argues that innovation flourishes best in environments that tolerate failure. - 拆解：based on... 修饰report；conducted over... 修饰interviews（后置定语链）；主干 argues that...；that tolerate failure 修饰environments。 - 译：这份基于三年访谈的报告认为，创新在容忍失败的环境中最为兴盛。

S24. Given the same resources, some schools produce dramatically better outcomes, suggesting that management matters as much as funding. - 拆解：Given... 条件分词；主干 some schools produce...；suggesting... 现在分词作结果状语；that从句作suggesting的宾语（as much as比较）。 - 译：在资源相同的情况下，一些学校的成效显著更好，这表明管理与资金同等重要。

S25. All flights having been canceled because of the storm, thousands of passengers were left stranded at the airport overnight. - 拆解：独立主格 All flights having been canceled 作原因；主干 passengers were left stranded。 - 译：由于航班因风暴全部取消，数千名旅客 overnight 滞留机场。

六、综合长难句（真题难度）

S26. The assumption that economic growth, provided it is sustained, will eventually benefit every segment of society has been challenged by evidence from economies that grew rapidly while inequality widened. - 拆解：主干 The assumption has been challenged by evidence；that economic growth... 同位语从句（内含provided条件插入）；that grew rapidly... 修饰economies。 - 译："只要经济持续增长，最终会让社会各阶层受益"这一假设，已受到"快速增长同时不平等加剧"的经济体证据的挑战。

S27. What the history of science teaches us is not that errors are avoidable, but that the correction of errors, when institutionalized, becomes the very engine of progress. - 拆解：What... 主语从句；not that A but that B 并列表语；when institutionalized 插入省略。 - 译：科学史教给我们的，不是错误可以避免，而是错误的纠正一旦制度化，就会成为进步本身的引擎。

S28. Had the authorities acted on the warnings issued years earlier, much of the damage that the flood inflicted on the region could have been avoided. - 拆解：Had... acted = If the authorities had acted（虚拟条件倒装）；主干 much of the damage could have been avoided；that the flood inflicted... 修饰damage。 - 译：如果当局早些年就这些警告采取行动，洪水对该地区造成的很大一部分损失本可避免。

S29. It is one thing to acknowledge that algorithms shape our choices, and quite another to demand that their designers be held accountable for consequences they neither intended nor foresaw. - 拆解：It is one thing to..., and quite another to...（固定对照结构）；demand that + (should) be held 虚拟语气；they neither intended nor foresaw 省略that/which的定语从句修饰consequences。 - 译：承认算法塑造我们的选择是一回事，要求其设计者对他们既无意也未能预见的后果负责，则完全是另一回事。

S30. Nations that treat education as an investment rather than an expenditure tend to discover, sometimes decades later, that the returns compound in ways no short-term metric can capture. - 拆解：that treat... 修饰nations；主干 tend to discover that...（宾语从句）；sometimes decades later 插入状语；in ways no short-term metric can capture（省略that/which）。 - 译：把教育当作投资而非支出的国家，往往会在——有时是几十年后——发现其回报以任何短期指标都无法衡量的方式复利增长。

使用方法

  1. 每天精析2句：先遮住拆解，自己划主干（谁—干什么），再对照。
  2. 划主干口诀：先找谓语动词（排除从句内的动词），向前找主语，向后看宾语/表语；剩下的从句逐层挂到主干上。
  3. 与《作文模板与阅读方法论.md》配套：S1-S30 的结构全部可以反向用于写作（拆解句=高级句型库）。
