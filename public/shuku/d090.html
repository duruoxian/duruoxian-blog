作文模板与阅读方法论

使用时机：2027年10月起正式套用（现在只混脸熟）。原则：模板搭骨架，考场填血肉——阅卷人最反感全套模板，本文件给的是"不会写死不了、会写拿高分"的框架句。

一、小作文（应用文，英一10分/英二10分）

格式铁律

  • 称呼：Dear ...,（顶格，逗号）
  • 正文：3段（目的→内容→期待回复/致谢）
  • 落款：Yours sincerely, Li Ming（右对齐；名字按题目要求）

六大类型模板句

1. 建议信（推荐） - I am writing to put forward some practical suggestions regarding... - To begin with, it would be advisable to... / In addition, ... would go a long way toward... - I hope you will find these suggestions constructive. Looking forward to your reply.

2. 推荐信 - It is my great pleasure to recommend ... to you. - What makes him/her an ideal candidate is that... / His/Her outstanding performance in ... speaks volumes. - I am convinced that he/she will live up to your expectations.

3. 邀请信 - On behalf of ..., I am honored to invite you to... - The details are as follows: ... / It would be our great honor if you could... - Please feel free to contact me at ... if you have any questions.

4. 感谢信 - I am writing to extend my heartfelt gratitude for... - But for your help, I could not have... - My appreciation is beyond words, and I hope to reciprocate in the near future.

5. 道歉信 - I am terribly sorry for... / Please accept my sincere apology for... - The reason for ... was that... / To make up for it, I would like to... - I assure you that such a thing will never happen again.

6. 通知（Notice） - 命题格式：标题 Notice 居中；落款单位+日期。 - ... is to be held in ... on ... / Those interested please sign up at ... before ...

二、大作文（英一图画作文15分）

三段式骨架（每段功能固定）

第一段 描述图画（3句）：As is vividly depicted in the picture, （描述核心人物/动作）. （补充细节：文字说明/对比）. Simple as the picture is, the implication it conveys is far-reaching.

第二段 寓意论证（5句）：The picture reveals a prevailing phenomenon that （主题句：把图画翻译成社会现象/品质）. / 举证：A case in point is that... / 因果：This can be attributed to..., which in turn... / 对比：By contrast, those who ... tend to... / 升华：It is the ... that distinguishes ... from ...

第三段 评论建议（3句）：From what has been discussed above, we may safely arrive at the conclusion that... / Accordingly, it is imperative for us to... / Only in this way can we expect a more promising future.

万能论证素材（背5个，考场上"以一敌百"）

  1. 个人成长：A case in point is young people who persevere in the face of setbacks are more likely to fulfill their potential.
  2. 社会热点：As is often the case in modern society, ... has drawn increasing public attention.
  3. 科技双刃剑：While technology facilitates..., it inevitably brings about...
  4. 数据论证（可虚构泛指）：According to a recent survey, a majority of respondents admit that...
  5. 名言嫁接：As a well-known saying goes, "...". This proverb illustrates that...

高级替换速查

think→contend/argue / important→crucial/paramount / many→a multitude of / more and more→an increasing number of / because→owing to/in that / so→therefore/consequently / but→nevertheless/whereas / very→considerably / good→beneficial / bad→detrimental。

三、英二图表作文（备选，若最终报专硕）

  • 首段：As is clearly shown in the chart, ... increased/decreased from A to B during the period...
  • 数字表达：account for / make up / stand at / witness a sharp rise。
  • 中段原因、尾段预测：The trend is likely to continue in the foreseeable future.

四、阅读六大题型方法论（英一40分的打法）

题型 识别 定位与打法
细节题 According to para.X... 题干关键词回文定位；正确答案=原文同义改写
主旨题 best title / mainly about 首末段+各段首句；选"覆盖全文范围"的，警惕以偏概全
推理题 infer / imply 答案=原文的就近推一步；推两步就是过度推理
词义题 "xx" (Line X) means 看前后句逻辑（同义解释/转折反推），不看词典义
态度题 attitude/tone 找形容词副词；中性客观词多为正解（objective/cautious），极端词慎选
例证题 the author mentions... to 例子服务于观点，答案在例子的前后句

干扰项四大套路（秒杀排错）

  1. 偷换概念：换主语/换范围（原文some→选项all）。
  2. 张冠李戴：A的观点安到B头上。
  3. 无中生有：原文没提的常识性"正确"选项。
  4. 正反混淆：把否定含义正向改写。

做题节奏

  • 一篇18分钟：先读题干（划定位词）→ 通读文章（首末段+段首句精读，其余扫读）→ 逐题定位 → 相邻两题答案通常顺序对应段落。

五、节奏安排

  • 2027.10 前：方法论过一遍 + 真题阅读里验证套路（不写作文）。
  • 10-11月：每周1篇小作文+1篇大作文（先抄模板再脱稿）；找研友互批或用批改工具。
  • 12月：背诵自己打磨好的"个人版模板"，每两天默写一套保持手感。
