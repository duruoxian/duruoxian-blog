英语 · 翻译实战（100句 · Part C 专项）

翻译10分=5句×2分，采分点是"拆分后的各小段"。本册100句按真题风格自编（2026-10-29 由20句扩至100句，第21-100句共16组），每句四件套：【句】【切分】（先按采分点断句）【评分点】（容易丢分处）【译文】。 训练法：每句先自己写→对照切分找漏点→抄一遍参考译文。与《长难句精析》《翻译_方法论与精练》配套。

第一组 科技类（5句）

  1. What distinguishes the current wave of artificial intelligence from earlier false starts is not merely faster computers, but the availability of vast quantities of data on which systems can be trained. 【切分】① What distinguishes A from B（主语从句）｜② is not merely…, but…（not…but并列表语）｜③ on which systems can be trained（介词+which定语从句，先行词data）。 【评分点】false starts 不能译"错误开始"，应译"失败的尝试/夭折的尝试"；on which 定语从句前置为"……的数据"。 【译文】当前这波人工智能浪潮与此前多次失败尝试的区别，不仅在于计算机速度更快，更在于拥有海量可供系统训练的数据。

  2. It is one thing to build a model that performs impressively in the laboratory; it is quite another to ensure that it behaves reliably in the messy conditions of the real world. 【切分】① It is one thing to…（形式主语）｜② it is quite another to…（对照结构）｜③ ensure that…（宾语从句）。 【评分点】It is one thing…quite another 固定对照句式="……是一回事，……完全是另一回事"。 【译文】造出一个在实验室里表现出色的模型是一回事；确保它在真实世界的复杂条件下稳定可靠，则完全是另一回事。

  3. The machines, having been fed millions of labeled examples, can now recognize patterns with an accuracy that sometimes exceeds that of trained professionals. 【切分】① having been fed…（完成式被动分词状语）｜② with an accuracy（介宾作状语）｜③ that exceeds that of…（定语从句+that替代accuracy）。 【评分点】第二个 that 是替代词（=the accuracy of），漏译"的准确率"会扣分。 【译文】这些机器在输入数百万个带标签的样本之后，如今识别模式的准确率有时甚至超过了训练有素的专业人员。

  4. Whether the technology will ultimately serve society or undermine it depends less on the code itself than on the choices of those who deploy it. 【切分】① Whether…or…（主语从句）｜② depends less on…than on…（比较结构）｜③ those who deploy it（先行词+定语从句）。 【评分点】less A than B 译"与其说取决于A，不如说取决于B"；those who=部署它的人。 【译文】这项技术最终是服务于社会还是损害社会，与其说取决于代码本身，不如说取决于部署它的人做出的选择。

  5. Progress in the field, impressive as it has been, should not distract attention from the stubborn problems that remain unsolved. 【切分】① impressive as it has been（as让步倒装：adj+as+主谓）｜② distract…from…｜③ that remain unsolved（定语从句）。 【评分点】as让步=倒装让步，译"尽管……"；impressive as it has been=尽管进展令人印象深刻。 【译文】尽管该领域的进展令人印象深刻，但它们不应转移人们对那些悬而未决的顽固问题的注意。

第二组 社会与经济类（5句）

  1. That the number of hours worked has fallen while output per hour has risen is the quietest of the many revolutions the past century has witnessed. 【切分】① That…and…（主语从句含while对比）｜② the quietest of…（最高级）｜③ the past century has witnessed（省略that的定语从句，修饰revolutions）。 【评分点】witness 的主语是时间（the past century）——"过去一个世纪见证的众多变革中最悄无声息的一场"。 【译文】工作小时数在下降、而每小时产出在上升——这是过去一个世纪所见证的众多变革中最悄无声息的一场。

  2. Cities that once competed to attract factories now compete for something scarcer: talented workers who can live and work anywhere they choose. 【切分】① Cities that…（定语从句）｜② compete for something scarcer（比较）｜③ who can live and work anywhere they choose（定语从句+地点状语从句省略that）。 【评分点】scarcer="更为稀缺的东西"；anywhere they choose="在其选定的任何地方"。 【译文】曾经竞相吸引工厂的城市，如今争夺的是更为稀缺的资源：能够在自己选定的任何地方生活与工作的优秀人才。

  3. The committee's report argues, with considerable evidence on its side, that the benefits of growth have been distributed far less evenly than the headline figures suggest. 【切分】① argues that…（宾语从句，被插入语隔开）｜② with considerable evidence on its side（插入状语）｜③ less evenly than…suggest（比较+从句）。 【评分点】headline figures="总体数字/表面数字"；on its side="有其依据"。 【译文】该委员会的报告认为——它手中掌握着相当多的证据——经济增长成果的分配，远没有总体数字所显示的那样均衡。

  4. Hardly a week passes without a study claiming that remote work boosts productivity, only for another study, months later, to reach the opposite conclusion. 【切分】① Hardly…without…（双重否定=几乎每周都）｜② claiming that…（伴随分词）｜③ only for…to…（"结果却"结构）。 【评分点】only for sb. to do="结果却……"，表达意外转折。 【译文】几乎每周都有一项研究声称远程办公能提高生产率，结果几个月后另一项研究却得出相反的结论。

  5. What looks like generosity in the short term may, on closer inspection, turn out to be an investment whose returns accrue to the giver as well as to the recipient. 【切分】① What looks like…（主语从句）｜② on closer inspection（插入）｜③ whose returns accrue to…（whose定语从句）。 【评分点】accrue to="归于、流向"；A as well as B="不仅B而且A"（重心在A）。 【译文】短期内看似慷慨的举动，细察之下可能是一种投资——其回报不仅流向受助者，也流向给予者。

第三组 历史与文化类（5句）

  1. Few inventions have reshaped daily life as thoroughly as electric lighting, which lengthened the usable day, redrew the boundaries between work and leisure, and quietly reorganized the architecture of cities. 【切分】① Few…as thoroughly as…（否定比较=几乎没有……像……这样彻底）｜② which lengthened…, redrew…, and reorganized…（三个并列谓语）。 【评分点】三并列动词都要译出；lengthened the usable day="延长了可用的一天"。 【译文】很少有发明像电灯这样彻底地重塑了日常生活：它延长了人们可用的时间，重新划定了工作与休闲的界线，并悄然重组了城市的格局。

  2. It was not until the standardization of time zones, driven largely by the needs of the railways, that ordinary people began to arrange their lives by the clock rather than by the sun. 【切分】① It was not until…that…（强调句，强调时间状语）｜② driven by…（分词插入）｜③ by the clock rather than by the sun（对比）。 【评分点】not until 强调句="直到……才……"；rather than="而不是"。 【译文】直到——主要由于铁路的需要——时区实现标准化之后，普通人才开始按照时钟而不是按照太阳来安排自己的生活。

  3. The letters, preserved by chance in an attic and unnoticed for two centuries, offer historians a glimpse of how ordinary families experienced events that textbooks compress into single sentences. 【切分】① preserved…and unnoticed…（两个过去分词定语）｜② a glimpse of how…（同位语+宾语从句）｜③ that textbooks compress…（定语从句）。 【评分点】by chance="偶然"；compress into single sentences="一笔带过"。 【译文】这些书信因偶然的机缘在阁楼中得以保存、两百年无人问津，它们让历史学家得以一窥普通家庭如何经历那些在教科书中被一笔带过的事件。

  4. Tradition, in this view, is less a museum piece to be preserved untouched than a living practice that each generation renegotiates with its own needs. 【切分】① less A than B（与其说A不如说B）｜② to be preserved untouched（不定式被动+过去分词补语）｜③ that each generation renegotiates（定语从句）。 【评分点】less…than…重心在B（"更是一种……的鲜活实践"）；renegotiate="重新协商、不断调适"。 【译文】依此观点，传统与其说是一件必须原封不动保存的博物馆藏品，不如说是一种鲜活的实践——每一代人都根据自身的需要与它重新协商。

  5. What the artifacts cannot tell us, and what written records often distort, is the texture of everyday life: what people ate, how they argued, and what they worried about after dark. 【切分】① What…and what…（两个并列主语从句）｜② is the texture of…（表语）｜③ 冒号后三个 what/how 并列（解释性列举）。 【评分点】texture="真实面貌/肌理"；after dark="入夜之后"，不能直译"黑暗后"。 【译文】器物无法告诉我们、而文字记载又常常歪曲的，是日常生活的真实肌理：人们吃什么，如何争吵，入夜后又为什么而忧心。

第四组 政策与治理类（5句）

  1. Regulations, however well intentioned, are only as effective as the machinery that enforces them, and it is here — in staffing, funding and independence — that most reforms fall short. 【切分】① however well intentioned（让步插入）｜② only as effective as…（同级比较）｜③ it is here that…（强调句强调状语）。 【评分点】only as effective as the machinery that enforces them="其效力取决于执行机制"；fall short="力有不逮、不到位"。 【译文】法规无论初衷多好，其效力都取决于执行它的机制——而正是在人员、经费与独立性这些环节上，大多数改革力有不逮。

  2. The evidence does not justify complacency; nor, however, does it support the alarmist claim that the sky is falling. 【切分】① not justify complacency（否定+名词）｜② nor does it…（否定倒装）｜③ claim that…（同位语从句）。 【评分点】complacency="自满/掉以轻心"；nor倒装="然而它也不支持……"。 【译文】这些证据并不足以让人高枕无忧；然而，它们同样不支持那种"天要塌了"的危言耸听。

  3. Governments that treat public consultation as a box-ticking exercise should not be surprised when citizens respond with cynicism. 【切分】① Governments that…（定语从句）｜② treat A as B（结构）｜③ when citizens respond with…（时间状语从句）。 【评分点】box-ticking exercise="走过场/应付了事"；cynicism="犬儒式的冷嘲/不信任"。 【译文】把公众咨询当作走过场的政府，不应惊讶于民众以不信任回应。

  4. Few policies succeed on the strength of design alone; most succeed, when they do, because their implementers were given the time and the discretion to adapt them to local conditions. 【切分】① Few…on the strength of…（否定主语）｜② when they do（插入省略=when they succeed）｜③ because…adapt them to…（原因从句）。 【评分点】on the strength of="单凭"；discretion="自主裁量权"。 【译文】很少有政策单凭设计就能成功；即便成功，多数也是因为执行者被给予了充足的时间和自主裁量权，得以因地制宜地加以调适。

  5. The question is no longer whether societies should harness these technologies, but on whose terms, under whose oversight, and for whose benefit. 【切分】① no longer whether…but…（not A but B扩展）｜② 三个并列介词短语（on whose terms / under whose oversight / for whose benefit）。 【评分点】三个 whose 并列排比必须全部译出且节奏一致："以谁的条件、受谁的监督、为谁的利益"。 【译文】问题已不再是社会是否应当利用这些技术，而是：以谁的条件、受谁的监督、为谁的利益来利用。

翻译评分点总结（自查清单）

  1. 切分：先竖线分段（逗号、连词、which/that处），每段0.5分上下；
  2. 疑难小词：as/that/it/which/whose 的指代必须译明；
  3. 固定句式：It is one thing…quite another / less A than B / not until强调 / nor倒装 / only for…to —— 见到即套模板；
  4. 不能直译的表达：headline figures（表面数字）、fall short（不足）、on its side（有其依据）、after dark（入夜后）；
  5. 译文定稿前通读一遍：中文通顺比英文忠实更先被扣分——先像话，再精确。

第五组 科技与网络类（5句）

  1. The smartphone has done more than any previous device to blur the line between work and private life, and the consequences of this blurring are only now becoming visible. 【切分】① has done more than any previous device to…（比较+不定式）｜② blur the line between A and B｜③ are only now becoming visible（进行时表渐进）。 【评分点】blur the line="模糊了……的界限"；more than any previous device="比以往任何设备都更"。 【译文】智能手机比以往任何设备都更彻底地模糊了工作与私人生活之间的界限，而这种模糊的后果如今才逐渐显现。

  2. It is tempting to assume that what can be measured will therefore be managed, yet the most important things in an organization often resist quantification. 【切分】① It is tempting to assume that…（形式主语）｜② what can be measured will be managed（主语从句）｜③ resist quantification（拟物用法）。 【评分点】tempting="诱人的（但常暗含不可取）"；resist quantification="难以量化"。 【译文】人们很容易以为凡是可测量的就必能被管理，然而一个组织中最重要的东西往往恰恰无法量化。

  3. The internet was celebrated, in its early years, as a force that would flatten hierarchies; two decades on, new platforms have built walls higher than the old ones. 【切分】① was celebrated as…（被动+as）｜② that would flatten hierarchies（定语从句+过去将来）｜③ two decades on（时间状语="二十年过去"）。 【评分点】flatten hierarchies="削平等级结构"；two decades on 独立状语别漏。 【译文】互联网早年曾被誉为一种将削平等级结构的力量；二十年过去，新的平台筑起了比旧高墙更高的墙。

  4. Whether an algorithm is "fair" depends not only on its design but on the data it is fed, and data, unlike mathematics, carries the fingerprints of history. 【切分】① Whether…depends not only on…but on…｜② it is fed（省略that/which的定语从句）｜③ unlike mathematics（插入比较）。 【评分点】be fed="被投喂/输入"；carry the fingerprints of="带有……的烙印"。 【译文】一个算法是否"公平"，不仅取决于它的设计，还取决于喂给它的数据——而数据不同于数学，它带着历史的指纹。

  5. What the engineers optimized for speed, the users experienced as impatience; the same feature read differently from opposite ends of the system. 【切分】① What…optimized for speed（主语从句）｜② experienced as…（被动感受）｜③ read differently（"被解读"）。 【评分点】read=被解读；from opposite ends of the system="从系统的两端看"。 【译文】工程师为速度所做的优化，在用户那里体验成了急躁；同一特性，站在系统的两端会读出截然不同的含义。

第六组 教育类（5句）

  1. A university that measures itself only by the salaries of its graduates will, over time, stop asking what its graduates are for. 【切分】① that measures…by…（定语从句）｜② over time（时间状语）｜③ what its graduates are for（宾语从句+be for="用途/意义"）。 【评分点】measure A by B="以B衡量A"；be for="存在的意义"。 【译文】一所只以毕业生薪资来衡量自身的大学，假以时日，将不再追问自己的毕业生究竟为何而存在。

  2. Learning that is always pleasant is rarely deep; the productive struggle against difficulty is not an obstacle to understanding but its very condition. 【切分】① that is always pleasant（定语从句）｜② not…but…（否肯并列）｜③ its very condition（very强调）。 【评分点】productive struggle="有益的挣扎/有成效的攻坚"；its very condition="恰恰是它的前提"。 【译文】总是轻松愉快的学习很少深刻；与困难的有益搏斗不是理解的障碍，而恰恰是理解得以发生的前提。

  3. The examination, designed to sort students fairly, ends by sorting them according to how well they can perform under the particular pressure it creates. 【切分】① designed to…（分词定语）｜② ends by doing（"最终以……收场"）｜③ how well they can perform…（宾语从句）｜④ it creates（定语从句省略that）。 【评分点】sort="筛选分等"；end by="最终落得"。 【译文】这场本为公平筛选学生而设计的考试，最终筛选出来的，是学生在它所制造的那种特定压力下的表现能力。

  4. What students remember years later is seldom the syllabus; it is the moment a teacher took their question seriously. 【切分】① What…remember（主语从句）｜② the moment（省略when的同位/时间用法）｜③ took…seriously。 【评分点】the moment a teacher took…="老师认真对待他们提问的那一刻"；took seriously=认真对待。 【译文】多年以后学生记住的很少是大纲内容；他们记住的，是某位老师认真对待他们提问的那一刻。

  5. Reformers who promise that technology will finally make teaching efficient have confused the transmission of information with the slow work of formation. 【切分】① who promise that…（定语从句+宾从）｜② confuse A with B（"把A与B混为一谈"）｜③ the transmission of…with the slow work of…（对仗）。 【评分点】transmission="传递信息"；formation="人的养成/塑造"——一对核心概念。 【译文】那些许诺技术终将使教学变得高效的改革者，把信息的传递与人的养成这一缓慢工作混为一谈了。

第七组 环境与城市类（5句）

  1. The city that grows upward rather than outward uses a fraction of the land per resident, yet its costs are hidden in concrete, steel and the patience of its builders. 【切分】① that grows upward rather than outward｜② a fraction of（"极小部分"）｜③ yet its costs are hidden in…（转折+被动）。 【评分点】a fraction of="只占……的零头"；hidden in="隐含在……之中"。 【译文】向上生长而非向外蔓延的城市，每个居民所占用的土地只是零头，但它的代价隐藏在混凝土、钢铁以及建设者的耐心里。

  2. Flood defenses built for the storms of the past are quietly failing against the storms of the present, and the gap will widen before it closes. 【切分】① built for…（分词定语）｜② failing against…（现在进行）｜③ before it closes（时间从句="在弥合之前"）。 【评分点】gap will widen before it closes="差距先扩大后缩小"——先恶化后好转的曲折。 【译文】为过去的暴雨而建的防洪设施，正悄然败给今天的暴雨，而这道缺口在弥合之前还会继续扩大。

  3. What makes a street walkable is not charm but a set of dull measurements: the distance between crossings, the width of pavements, the speed of traffic. 【切分】① What makes…（主语从句）｜② not A but B｜③ 冒号后三个并列名词短语（解释列举）。 【评分点】dull="枯燥的（但对的）"；crossings="过街路口"。 【译文】让一条街道宜于步行的是一套枯燥的指标，而不是风情：路口之间的距离、人行道的宽度、车流的速度。

  4. Species that cannot move fast enough are not merely at risk; they are already being replaced by whatever can tolerate the new conditions. 【切分】① that cannot move fast enough（定语从句）｜② not merely（递进）｜③ whatever can tolerate…（宾语从句=任何能够……的物种）。 【评分点】whatever=凡是能够……的东西；already being replaced="已经正在被取代"。 【译文】迁移得不够快的物种不只是面临危险；它们已经在被任何能够容忍新环境的物种所取代。

  5. The cheapest kilowatt is the one never generated, a truth that efficiency campaigns keep rediscovering and budgets keep forgetting. 【切分】① the one never generated（省略which is的定语）｜② a truth that…（同位语+定语从句）｜③ keep doing（"一再"）。 【评分点】a truth that…"这一真理"要译出同位关系；keep forgetting="不断遗忘"。 【译文】最便宜的电力是永远不必发出来的电——这是一个效率运动一再重新发现、而预算部门不断遗忘的真理。

第八组 心理与行为类（5句）

  1. People do not experience their lives as a sequence of years but as a collection of moments, and memory, not arithmetic, decides which moments count. 【切分】① not as A but as B（结构）｜② a collection of moments（"若干瞬间集合"）｜③ memory, not arithmetic, decides（插入否定）。 【评分点】count="算数/重要"；not arithmetic="而不是算术（平均计算）"。 【译文】人们并不把自己的生活体验为一年年的流逝，而是一连串瞬间的集合；决定哪些瞬间算数的，是记忆而不是算术。

  2. The habit of postponing difficult decisions is rarely laziness; more often it is the mind protecting itself from a choice that cannot be unmade. 【切分】① is rarely laziness（否定表语）｜② more often（比较递进）｜③ that cannot be unmade（定语从句=无法撤销）。 【评分点】unmake="撤销、反悔"；protecting itself from="自我保护以免于"。 【译文】拖延困难决定的习惯很少是因为懒惰；更多时候，那是心灵在保护自己，免于做出一个无法撤销的选择。

  3. Attention, once scattered across a dozen screens, does not return to its former depth merely because the screens are closed. 【切分】① once scattered…（分词状语）｜② does not return…merely because…（否定+原因）。 【评分点】its former depth="原有的深度"；merely="仅仅因为"。 【译文】注意力一旦被十几块屏幕打散，并不会因为屏幕关上就恢复原有的深度。

  4. Confidence built on praise alone collapses at the first serious setback; confidence built on survived difficulties quietly compounds. 【切分】① built on…（两个分词定语对比）｜② collapses at…（"在……面前崩溃"）｜③ compounds（不及物="复利式增长"）。 【评分点】survived difficulties="熬过去的困难"；compound="复利增长"。 【译文】单靠夸奖建立的自信会在第一次严重挫折时崩塌；而靠熬过困难积累的自信，会安静地复利增长。

  5. We forgive others their flaws more easily than we forgive ourselves ours, which is why advice is cheap and change is expensive. 【切分】① forgive sb. sth.（双宾）｜② than we forgive ourselves ours（对比省略：ours=our flaws）｜③ which is why…（非限定定语从句指代整句）。 【评分点】ours 补全为"自己的缺点"才通顺；which 指代前面整件事。 【译文】我们原谅他人的缺点远比原谅自己的容易——这正是为什么建议总是廉价，而改变总是昂贵。

第九组 医学与公共健康类（5句）

  1. Medicine has become remarkably good at postponing death; whether it has become correspondingly good at making those postponed years worth living is a harder question. 【切分】① good at postponing（"擅长推迟"）｜② whether…is a harder question（主语从句）｜③ worth living（形容词短语补语）。 【评分点】postponing death="推迟死亡"；worth living="值得过"。 【译文】医学已变得极擅长推迟死亡；至于它是否同样擅长让这些被推迟的岁月过得值得，则是一个更难的问题。

  2. A drug that helps the average patient may still fail the individual in front of you, and statistics, however necessary, are silent about persons. 【切分】① that helps…may still fail…｜② however necessary（让步插入）｜③ are silent about（"对……保持沉默/无法言说"）。 【评分点】fail="辜负、无效于"；silent about persons="对具体的个人无话可说"。 【译文】一种对普通病人有效的药物，仍可能对你面前的这个个体无效；而统计数字再必要，对具体的个人也是沉默的。

  3. Public health wins its victories in statistics rather than in stories: the epidemic that never happened makes no news. 【切分】① in A rather than in B（对比）｜② 冒号后解释句｜③ that never happened（定语从句）。 【评分点】the epidemic that never happened="未曾发生的那场疫情"——预防的悖论。 【译文】公共卫生的胜利体现在统计数字里而非故事里：从未发生的那场疫情，成不了新闻。

  4. The body keeps score in ways no questionnaire fully captures, which is why the patient's own account remains evidence of the first importance. 【切分】① in ways no questionnaire captures（"以问卷无法完全捕捉的方式"）｜② which is why…（指代前句）｜③ of the first importance="头等重要"。 【评分点】keep score="记账/留下记录"；evidence of the first importance="第一等重要的证据"。 【译文】身体会以任何问卷都无法完全捕捉的方式留下记录——这正是为什么患者本人的叙述始终是头等重要的证据。

  5. Progress in surgery has lowered the risk of any single operation while raising the number of operations a typical life now contains. 【切分】① has lowered…while raising…（while对比分词）｜② the number of operations a typical life contains（定语从句省略that）。 【评分点】any single operation="任何单次手术"；a typical life="普通人的一生"。 【译文】外科进步降低了任何单次手术的风险，却也提高了普通人一生所要经历的手术次数。

第十组 经济与工作类（5句）

  1. Automation has not so much eliminated work as redistributed it, from muscles to screens, from factories to warehouses whose floors are silent until the shift changes. 【切分】① not so much A as B（"与其说A不如说B"）｜② from A to B, from C to D（双重转移）｜③ whose floors are silent（定语从句）。 【评分点】not so much…as…"与其说……不如说"；silent until the shift changes="换班之前一直静悄悄"。 【译文】自动化与其说消灭了工作，不如说重新分配了工作：从肌肉转向屏幕，从工厂转向仓库——在换班之前，那里的地面静悄悄。

  2. The market rewards scarcity, and in an economy where information is abundant, the scarce goods are judgment, trust and the patience to see projects through. 【切分】① and 并列+where定语从句｜② the scarce goods are…（表语列举）｜③ see projects through（"把项目做完"）。 【评分点】scarce goods="稀缺品"；see…through="坚持完成"。 【译文】市场奖赏稀缺；在一个信息过剩的经济体里，稀缺的正是判断力、信任，以及把项目坚持做完的耐心。

  3. Wages have risen on paper, but paper is not rent, and the distance between the two is where the politics of the next decade will be fought. 【切分】① on paper（"账面上"）｜② paper is not rent（隐喻："数字不是生活成本"）｜③ where…will be fought（表语从句="战场所在"）。 【评分点】the distance between the two="两者之间的差距"；fought="争夺"。 【译文】工资在账面上涨了，但账面不是房租——这两者之间的差距，正是未来十年政治角力的战场。

  4. Companies speak of loyalty to their customers, yet the devices they sell are engineered to be replaced before they are understood. 【切分】① speak of（"宣称/谈到"）｜② yet转折+they sell（插入定语）｜③ be engineered to="被设计成"｜④ before they are understood（"还没弄懂就已……"）。 【评分点】engineered to="从设计上就……"；before="还没……就……"。 【译文】公司口口声声忠于顾客，可它们卖出的设备从设计上就是要在你还没弄懂它之前就被更换掉。

  5. Economic growth, once a promise shared by all classes, now asks each household to decide what it is willing to trade for it. 【切分】① once a promise shared by…（同位语）｜② ask sb. to do（"要求"）｜③ what it is willing to trade for it（宾语从句）。 【评分点】trade A for B="用A换B"；it is willing to="它愿意"。 【译文】经济增长曾是一份属于所有阶层的承诺，如今却要求每个家庭自己去决定：愿意用什么去交换它。

第十一组 历史与记忆类（5句）

  1. Nations do not merely remember their past; they curate it, deciding which events stand in the square and which are filed in the basement. 【切分】① not merely…（递进）｜② curate（"策展"隐喻）｜③ which…and which…（并列宾从）。 【评分点】curate="策展/挑选陈列"；filed in the basement="归档进地下室"。 【译文】国家不只是记住自己的过去；它们策划过去——决定哪些事件矗立在广场上，哪些被归档进地下室。

  2. The generation that lived through the events rarely wrote the textbooks; memory was first preserved, then standardized, and finally administered. 【切分】① that lived through（"亲历"）｜② rarely wrote（否定）｜③ first…then…and finally…（三段递进）。 【评分点】live through="亲历"；administered="被管理/被规范化"。 【译文】亲历事件的那个年代很少去写教科书；记忆先是得到保存，继而走向标准化，最终被纳入管理。

  3. Monuments promise permanence, and precisely for that reason they become the first places where changing values are negotiated. 【切分】① promise permanence（"许诺永恒"）｜② precisely for that reason（强调）｜③ where…are negotiated（定语从句）。 【评分点】precisely="恰恰"；negotiated="被重新协商"。 【译文】纪念碑许诺永恒，而恰恰因为这一点，它们最先成为新旧价值观念交锋协商的场所。

  4. History written by the victors is a cliché; more often it is history written by whoever kept the paperwork. 【切分】① a cliché（表语="老生常谈"）｜② more often（比较递进）｜③ whoever kept the paperwork（宾语从句="保管文书的人"）。 【评分点】cliché="陈词滥调"；paperwork="档案文书"。 【译文】"历史由胜利者书写"已是老生常谈；更常见的情况是——历史由碰巧保管文书的人书写。

  5. What a society chooses to forget is often more revealing than what it remembers, and archives burn twice: once by accident, once by decision. 【切分】① What…and what…（并列主语从句）｜② more revealing than（比较）｜③ burn twice: once…once…（排比解释）。 【评分点】revealing="更能说明问题的"；burn twice="被烧两次（意外一次、有意一次）"。 【译文】一个社会选择遗忘什么，往往比它记住什么更能说明问题；而档案总要被烧两次——一次出于意外，一次出于决定。

第十二组 法律与制度类（5句）

  1. A law that cannot be explained to the person it governs forfeits the consent on which all enforcement ultimately rests. 【切分】① that cannot be explained to…｜② the person it governs（定语从句双层）｜③ forfeit="丧失"｜④ on which…rests（介词前置定语从句）。 【评分点】forfeit="丧失"；rest on="依赖于"。 【译文】一条无法向它所管辖的人解释清楚的法律，就丧失了所有执法最终赖以成立的同意。

  2. Rights are cheaper to proclaim than to protect, and the gap between the two is measured in courtrooms, budgets and patience. 【切分】① cheaper to A than to B｜② the gap between the two｜③ is measured in…（"以……来计量"）。 【评分点】proclaim="宣告"；measured in courtrooms="用法庭、预算和耐心来计量"。 【译文】宣告权利比保护权利便宜得多，而两者之间的差距，要用法庭、预算和耐心来计量。

  3. Precedent, whatever its philosophical weaknesses, gives citizens a map of tomorrow drawn from the decisions of yesterday. 【切分】① whatever its weaknesses（让步）｜② a map of tomorrow（隐喻）｜③ drawn from…（分词定语）。 【评分点】precedent="判例"；drawn from yesterday's decisions="由昨天的判决绘成"。 【译文】判例在法理上或有种种缺陷，但它给了公民一张用昨天的判决绘成的明日地图。

  4. Reform that arrives only after every interested party has been consulted tends to arrive after the problem has been redefined. 【切分】① that arrives only after…（定语从句+时间）｜② tend to arrive after…（呼应重复 arrive）｜③ redefined（"被重新定义"）。 【评分点】两个 after 的呼应："等所有相关方都咨询完，问题本身早已变了样"。 【译文】直到每个利益相关方都被咨询过才到来的改革，往往到来时，问题早已被重新定义过了。

  5. The constitution is not a suicide pact, but neither is it a menu from which each generation orders only what it likes. 【切分】① not A, but neither is B（双重否定平衡）｜② a menu from which…（介词前置定语从句）｜③ orders only what it likes（点餐隐喻）。 【评分点】suicide pact="自杀协定"（固定说法）；menu="菜单"——宪法不是可以随意挑选的。 【译文】宪法不是一份自杀协定，但它也不是一份菜单，容不得每一代人只点自己喜欢的那几样。

第十三组 哲学与思想类（5句）

  1. Doubt, in the scientific temper, is not a weakness to be concealed but a discipline to be practiced, refined and occasionally enjoyed. 【切分】① not a weakness…but a discipline（否肯并列）｜② to be concealed / to be practiced（三个不定式被动排比）。 【评分点】scientific temper="科学精神/气质"；practice a discipline="践行一种纪律"。 【译文】在科学精神之中，怀疑不是需要遮掩的弱点，而是一门需要践行、打磨乃至偶尔乐在其中的纪律。

  2. The question is not what we know, but what we are entitled to claim we know, and the distance between the two is where philosophy lives. 【切分】① not what…but what we are entitled to claim…（多重从句嵌套）｜② be entitled to="有资格"｜③ where…lives（表语从句）。 【评分点】entitled to claim="有资格声称"；the distance between the two="两者之间的距离"。 【译文】问题不在于我们知道什么，而在于我们有什么资格声称自己知道——这两者之间的距离，正是哲学栖身之处。

  3. An idea that explains everything explains nothing, and the habit of unfalsifiable thinking is harder to break than any factual error. 【切分】① that explains everything…nothing（悖论式对比）｜② unfalsifiable="不可证伪的"｜③ harder to break than…（比较）。 【评分点】explains nothing="什么都解释不了"；unfalsifiable="不可证伪"。 【译文】一个能解释一切的理论其实什么也没解释；不可证伪式思维的积习，比任何事实错误都更难纠正。

  4. Meaning is not found ready-made in the world; it is built, and like all construction it leaves scaffolding that others must eventually dismantle. 【切分】① not found ready-made（过去分词作主语补语）｜② and like all construction（类比插入）｜③ that others must eventually dismantle（定语从句）。 【评分点】ready-made="现成的"；scaffolding="脚手架（比喻过渡性的支撑）"。 【译文】意义不是在世界上现成捡到的；它是被建造出来的——而一切建造都会留下脚手架，等着他人最终拆除。

  5. The examined life is slower and less comfortable, which is exactly why those who choose it owe the rest of us an explanation in plain words. 【切分】① The examined life（苏格拉底典故"经过审视的生活"）｜② which is exactly why…（指代整句）｜③ owe sb. sth.（双宾）。 【评分点】examined life="经过省察的生活"；owe an explanation="欠一个解释"。 【译文】经过省察的生活更缓慢、更不安逸——这恰恰是为什么，选择这种生活的人欠我们其他人一个用大白话给出的解释。

第十四组 新闻与信息类（5句）

  1. News that travels fastest is news that has been trimmed until nothing heavy remains, and accuracy is almost always the heaviest cargo. 【切分】① that travels fastest（定语从句）｜② trimmed until…（"被修剪到……"）｜③ almost always the heaviest（暗喻）。 【评分点】trimmed="被修剪/删削"；heavy cargo="沉重的货物"（准确性最容易被删）。 【译文】传播最快的新闻，是那些被修剪到没有任何分量的新闻——而准确性几乎永远是最重的那件行李。

  2. The reader who checks every claim is a myth; the practical question is which institutions make checking unnecessary for most people most of the time. 【切分】① who checks…（定语从句）｜② a myth（表语="神话/不存在"）｜③ which institutions make…unnecessary（宾语从句）。 【评分点】a myth="根本不存在"；make checking unnecessary="让核查变得不必要"。 【译文】每条信息都亲自核查的读者只是一个神话；现实的问题在于：哪些机构能让大多数人在大多数时候不必核查。

  3. Outrage travels well because it requires no context, and context is precisely what a feed is built to remove. 【切分】① because…（原因从句）｜② precisely what…（强调句型 what从句）｜③ a feed="信息流"。 【评分点】requires no context="不需要背景就能传播"；feed="（算法）信息流"。 【译文】愤怒最易传播，因为它不需要任何背景——而背景恰恰是信息流被设计出来要剔除的东西。

  4. An error retracted on page twelve reaches a fraction of the audience that read the accusation on page one. 【切分】① retracted on page twelve（分词定语="在第12版更正"）｜② a fraction of（"零头"）｜③ that read…（定语从句）。 【评分点】retract="撤回/更正"；a fraction of the audience="读者中的零头"。 【译文】刊登在第十二版上的更正，所能到达的读者，只是第一天在头版读到指控的人中的零头。

  5. Journalism at its best is not the first draft of history but its table of contents, pointing to where the real chapters are being written. 【切分】① at its best（"在其最佳状态"）｜② not…but…（否肯）｜③ pointing to…（伴随分词）｜④ where…are being written（宾语从句+进行被动）。 【评分点】first draft of history="历史的第一稿"（新闻业名言）；table of contents="目录"。 【译文】新闻业最好的样子，不是历史的初稿，而是历史的目录——它指示着真正的章节正在何处被书写。

第十五组 语言与翻译类（5句）

  1. A language is not a neutral container of thoughts; each tongue carves the world along its own grooves, and translation is the art of bridging carved surfaces. 【切分】① not a neutral container（否定隐喻）｜② carve…along its own grooves（"沿自己的纹路雕凿"）｜③ bridging…（动名词表语）。 【评分点】carve="雕凿/切分"；grooves="沟槽/纹路"。 【译文】语言不是思想的透明容器；每种语言都沿着自己的纹路雕凿世界，而翻译就是在两块雕痕各异的面之间架桥的艺术。

  2. The translator's first loyalty is to the reader's comprehension, which is why the most faithful translation is not always the most literal one. 【切分】① first loyalty to…（"首要忠诚对象"）｜② which is why…（指代）｜③ not always the most literal（比较否定）。 【评分点】literal="逐字直译"；faithful="忠实的"——两个"忠实"的分野。 【译文】译者的第一忠诚是读者的理解——这正是为什么最忠实的译文并不总是最字面的那一种。

  3. Every idiom is a fossil of a metaphor that a language has worn smooth, and translating it literally is exhibiting the stone instead of the carving. 【切分】① a fossil of a metaphor（"隐喻的化石"）｜② worn smooth（"被磨光滑"）｜③ exhibit A instead of B（"展示A而非B"）。 【评分点】wear smooth="磨得光滑"；exhibit the stone="把石头当展品（而丢了雕刻）"。 【译文】每个习语都是一块被语言磨得光滑的隐喻化石；逐字翻译它，等于把石头摆上展台，却弄丢了石上的雕刻。

  4. Grammar is less a set of rules than a set of habits, and habits, unlike statutes, can be broken without trial by anyone who knows why they exist. 【切分】① less A than B（重心在B）｜② unlike statutes（插入对比）｜③ without trial="不经审判"（法律双关）。 【评分点】statutes="成文法"；break without trial="随意打破（法律双关）"。 【译文】语法与其说是一套规则，不如说是一套习惯；习惯不同于成文法——知道它为何存在的人，无须"审判"即可打破它。

  5. To learn a second language as an adult is to rediscover, from the outside, machinery one has been operating blindly all one's life. 【切分】① To learn…is to rediscover…（不定式平行）｜② from the outside（"从外部"）｜③ machinery one has been operating（省略that的定语从句+现在完成进行）。 【评分点】machinery="机器/机制"；operate blindly="一直盲目操作"。 【译文】成年后学习第二语言，就是从外部重新发现自己一生都在盲目操作的那台机器。

第十六组 个人与社会类（5句）

  1. The self is not a possession to be defended but a project to be revised, and revisions are cheaper the earlier they are attempted. 【切分】① not a possession…but a project（否肯）｜② to be defended / to be revised（不定式被动排比）｜③ the earlier…the cheaper（比较级呼应结构）。 【评分点】possession="私产"；revision="修订"；the earlier…the cheaper="越早越便宜"。 【译文】自我不是需要捍卫的私产，而是有待修订的工程；而修订这件事，动手越早，代价越小。

  2. Friendship in adulthood is not maintained by intensity but by low-frequency reliability: the message that needs no reply, the visit that needs no occasion. 【切分】① not by A but by B｜② low-frequency reliability（自造复合词="低频但可靠"）｜③ 冒号后两个并列名词短语（the message/the visit）。 【评分点】low-frequency="不常联系"；needs no occasion="不需要什么由头"。 【译文】成年人的友谊靠的不是浓烈，而是低频的可靠：一条不必回复的消息，一次不需要由头的登门。

  3. The bravest thing most people do is not dramatic; it is continuing to show up for ordinary days after the version of life they planned has quietly lapsed. 【切分】① not dramatic（否定）｜② continuing to show up for…（动名词）｜③ after the version…has lapsed（时间从句）。 【评分点】show up="照常出现/露面"；lapse="悄然失效"。 【译文】多数人做过的最勇敢的事并不壮烈：在自己设想的人生版本悄然失效之后，仍然照常出现在一个个平凡的日子里。

  4. Money buys options rather than happiness, and the art lies in noticing which options are actually worth exercising. 【切分】① A rather than B（"是A而非B"）｜② the art lies in（"艺术在于"）｜③ worth exercising（="值得行使（期权隐喻）"）。 【评分点】options="选项/期权"（双关）；exercise="行使"。 【译文】金钱买到的是选项，而不是幸福；真正的功夫，在于看出哪些选项其实值得行使。

  5. A society can be judged by how it treats the questions it cannot yet answer, and by whether curiosity there is treated as fuel or as a threat. 【切分】① be judged by A and by B（双重标准）｜② the questions it cannot answer（定语从句）｜③ whether A or B（宾语从句）。 【评分点】cannot yet answer="尚无法回答"；treated as fuel or as a threat="被当作燃料还是威胁"。 【译文】衡量一个社会，可以看它如何对待自己还答不上来的问题，看那里的好奇心被当作燃料还是威胁。

第十七组 科学方法类（5句）

  1. A theory that survives every attempt to break it earns its place not through authority but through the accumulated patience of its attackers. 【切分】① that survives…（定语从句）｜② not through…but through…（介词并列）｜③ accumulated patience of its attackers（"攻击者们积累的耐心"）。 【评分点】survive="经受住"；attackers="试图推翻它的人"。 【译文】一个经受住所有推翻尝试的理论，其地位并非来自权威，而是来自攻击者们日积月累的耐心。

  2. Measurement did not merely sharpen observation; it changed what counted as an observation at all. 【切分】① not merely sharpen（否定递进）｜② what counted as…（宾语从句="什么才算"）。 【评分点】count as="被算作"；at all="根本"。 【译文】测量不只是让观察更锐利；它改变了"什么才配称作观察"本身。

  3. The map of science is redrawn not by disproving the whole territory but by finding the one road that does not quite reach the town it claims to reach. 【切分】① not by A but by B（介词并列）｜② the one road that…（定语从句）｜③ the town it claims to reach（双层定语）。 【评分点】redraw="重绘"；does not quite reach="并未完全抵达"。 【译文】科学版图的改绘，靠的不是推翻整片疆域，而是找到那条与它声称要抵达的城镇差了一步的路。

  4. Peer review is a filter, not a verdict; passing through it means a claim is worth discussing, not that it is true. 【切分】① A, not B（同位否定）｜② passing through it（动名词主语）｜③ means A, not that B（并列宾语从句）。 【评分点】verdict="终审判决"；worth discussing="值得讨论"。 【译文】同行评审是一道过滤网，不是一纸终审；通过评审只意味着一个主张值得讨论，而不是它为真。

  5. What gets funded gets studied, and what gets studied gets measured — a chain that quietly decides what a generation will know. 【切分】① What gets…gets…（排比主语从句）｜② a chain that…（同位语+定语从句）｜③ quietly decides（"悄然决定"）。 【评分点】gets funded="拿到经费"；a chain="这条链条"。 【译文】拿到经费的才有人研究，有人研究的才被测量——这条链条悄然决定了一代人将会知道什么。

第十八组 艺术与审美类（5句）

  1. A photograph does not show what happened; it shows what the photographer decided had happened, which is a different and more instructive thing. 【切分】① does not show…but shows…（否定+宾语从句）｜② decided had happened（过去完成时嵌套）｜③ which is…（非限定从句）。 【评分点】decided had happened="他认定已经发生的"；instructive="更有教益"。 【译文】照片展示的并非发生了什么，而是摄影者认定已经发生了什么——这是另一回事，也更有教益。

  2. Style, in the end, is what remains when every fashionable device has been tried and discarded. 【切分】① what remains（表语从句）｜② when…has been tried and discarded（时间从句+两个被动并列）。 【评分点】fashionable device="时髦手法"；discarded="被丢弃"。 【译文】风格，说到底是把一切时髦手法都试过并丢弃之后剩下的东西。

  3. The genius of great architecture is that it constrains its occupants gently, the way a well-made chair makes sitting upright feel like rest. 【切分】① is that…（表语从句）｜② constrains…gently（"温和地约束"）｜③ the way…（方式状语从句="就像"）｜④ feel like rest。 【评分点】constrains="约束"；the way="正如、就像"。 【译文】伟大建筑的天才之处在于它温和地约束着居住者——就像一把好椅子，让人挺直脊背也像在休息。

  4. An audience forgives a great deal to a work that is honest about one true thing, and almost nothing to a work that is fluent about everything. 【切分】① forgives A to B（"宽恕B的A"）｜② honest about one true thing vs fluent about everything（对仗）｜③ almost nothing（强烈对比）。 【评分点】forgive="宽恕/容忍"；fluent="流畅圆滑"。 【译文】观众对一件诚实说出一件真相的作品可以宽容许多，而对一件处处圆滑的作品几乎毫无宽容。

  5. Art does not console by pretending the wound is absent; it consoles by making the wound legible, and legibility is a kind of company. 【切分】① by pretending…（方式状语+宾从）｜② making A legible（"让A可以被读清楚"）｜③ a kind of company（"一种陪伴"）。 【评分点】console="抚慰"；legible="清晰可读"；company="陪伴"。 【译文】艺术的抚慰不在于假装伤口不存在，而在于把伤口写得让人能读懂——而"能被读懂"本身就是一种陪伴。

第十九组 数字生活类（5句）

  1. The feed does not reflect your interests; it shapes them, and the difference between reflecting and shaping is where a decade of policy debate has been stuck. 【切分】① not reflect…but shape（否肯）｜② the difference between A and B（主语）｜③ where…has been stuck（表语从句）。 【评分点】feed="信息流"；be stuck="陷入僵局"。 【译文】信息流并不反映你的兴趣，它在塑造你的兴趣——"反映"与"塑造"之间的差别，正是十年政策争论原地打转的地方。

  2. Convenience is never free; it is purchased with data, and the invoice arrives years later, itemized in ways no one reads. 【切分】① never free（否定）｜② purchased with data（被动）｜③ the invoice arrives…（隐喻贯穿）｜④ itemized in ways…（分词定语）。 【评分点】invoice="账单"；itemized="逐项列明"。 【译文】便利从来不是免费的，它用数据支付——账单多年后才寄到，逐项列明，却无人细读。

  3. A recommendation accepted without thought is a small vote cast for a world arranged by whoever wrote the ranking. 【切分】① accepted without thought（分词定语）｜② a small vote cast for…（"投给……的一票"）｜③ arranged by whoever wrote the ranking（过去分词+宾语从句）。 【评分点】cast a vote="投票"；ranking="排序榜单"。 【译文】不加思索就接受的每一条推荐，都是在为"由排榜之人安排的世界"投下一张小小的选票。

  4. The most private decisions — whom to trust, what to fear, whom to love — are now made with assistance whose costs are distributed invisibly. 【切分】① 破折号插入三个并列疑问词短语｜② made with assistance（"在协助下做出"）｜③ whose costs are distributed invisibly（定语从句）。 【评分点】with assistance="借助（算法的）协助"；distributed invisibly="以不可见的方式分摊"。 【译文】最私人的决定——信谁、怕什么、爱谁——如今都是在某种协助下做出的，而这种协助的代价以不可见的方式摊派着。

  5. Digital memory is cheap, and that is precisely the problem: a self that cannot forget is a self that cannot revise. 【切分】① cheap, and that is precisely the problem（转折强调）｜② a self that cannot forget（定语从句排比）｜③ revise="修订"。 【评分点】precisely="恰恰"；revise="修订自我"。 【译文】数字记忆太便宜了，而这恰恰是问题所在：一个无法遗忘的自我，也是一个无法修订的自我。

第二十组 综合收束类（5句）

  1. Every generation believes it stands at the hinge of history; the honest ones add that hinges are only visible from the far side. 【切分】① believes it stands at…（省略that的宾从）｜② the hinge of history（"历史的枢纽"）｜③ visible from the far side="从另一侧才能看清"。 【评分点】hinge="枢纽/合页"；the honest ones="诚实的那几代"。 【译文】每一代人都相信自己正站在历史的枢纽上；诚实的那几代还会补一句——枢纽这东西，要走到另一侧才看得见。

  2. The measure of a tool is not what it makes possible but what it makes easy, for ease is what quietly rewrites habit. 【切分】① not what…but what…（两个what从句对比）｜② for（并列连词="因为"）｜③ rewrite habit（"改写习惯"）。 【评分点】makes easy="使之变得容易"；for="因为"（书面）。 【译文】衡量一件工具，不在于它使什么成为可能，而在于它使什么变得容易——因为"容易"才悄悄改写习惯。

  3. Optimism and pessimism are both predictions, and predictions are cheaper than preparation. 【切分】① both predictions（同位）｜② cheaper than preparation（比较）。 【评分点】preparation="做打算/准备"；cheap="廉价（贬义）"。 【译文】乐观与悲观都只是预测，而预测——无论哪一种——都比实打实的准备廉价。

  4. What we call progress is a bet that today's conveniences will outweigh tomorrow's bills, and no one alive has yet seen the ledger closed. 【切分】① What we call…（主语从句）｜② a bet that…（同位语从句）｜③ outweigh="超过"｜④ the ledger closed="账本合上/结算"。 【评分点】bet="赌注"；ledger="总账"；outweigh="超过"。 【译文】我们所谓进步，是一场赌注：赌今天的便利终将压过明天的账单——而活着的人中，还没有谁见过这本总账合上结清。

  5. In the end, the most radical act available to an individual may be the oldest one: to pay attention, deliberately, to a world that profits from our distraction. 【切分】① the most radical act available to…（形容词后置定语）｜② the oldest one（对比）｜③ to pay attention, deliberately, to…（插入副词）｜④ that profits from our distraction（定语从句）。 【评分点】radical="激进/根本性的"；profit from="从中获利"；deliberately="有意识地"。 【译文】归根到底，对个人而言最根本的行动可能也是最古老的那个：有意识地、专注地看这个世界——尽管这个世界正靠我们的分心牟利。
