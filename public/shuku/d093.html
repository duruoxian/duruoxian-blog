写作范文 10 篇（英一大作文5篇 + 小作文5篇）

用法：先读范文划出"模板句"，再仿写同题；最后背自己改过的版本（个人版模板）。每篇均按考场时间 30分钟内可完成的篇幅书写。

大作文（图画作文，160-200词）

范文1 主题：坚持（一人挖井半途而废 vs 一人坚持到底）

As is vividly depicted in the picture, two young men are digging wells at different spots. One keeps moving to a new place whenever he fails to find water within a few strikes, while the other, despite digging only a few pits, persists in one spot until water gushes out. Simple as the picture is, the implication it conveys is far-reaching: perseverance, rather than constant change, is the key to success.

The picture reveals a prevailing phenomenon among young people: many of them abandon their goals halfway, blaming the difficulty of the task instead of their own lack of persistence. A case in point is that numerous students start learning a foreign language with great enthusiasm, yet only a minority stick to it long enough to achieve fluency. This can be attributed to the fact that immediate rewards are scarce on the road to any meaningful achievement, and those who cannot tolerate temporary frustration are doomed to end up with nothing. By contrast, history has repeatedly proved that extraordinary accomplishments, from scientific breakthroughs to athletic records, are the fruits of sustained effort.

From what has been discussed above, we may safely conclude that persistence is an indispensable quality on the road to success. Accordingly, it is imperative for young people to set a clear goal and stick to it. Only in this way can we expect to dig out the "water of success" beneath our own feet.

范文2 主题：合作（两双人协作夹起食物）

As is vividly shown in the picture, several people are sitting around a huge bowl of food, each holding a long spoon. Those who try to feed themselves cannot reach their mouths, whereas those who feed each other are enjoying the meal. The cartoonist intends to tell us that cooperation, rather than self-centered effort, is the way to mutual benefit.

In an era characterized by increasingly complex tasks, no individual, however talented, can accomplish everything alone. What the picture mirrors is a simple but often-forgotten truth: helping others is, in the long run, helping oneself. Take scientific research as an example: breakthroughs today are almost always the product of teams whose members share knowledge and divide labor. By contrast, excessive competition and mutual suspicion consume energy that should have been devoted to creation.

Accordingly, the spirit of teamwork deserves our earnest promotion. Schools should encourage students to work on group projects, and companies ought to reward collaboration as well as individual performance. Only when the culture of cooperation takes root can both individuals and the whole society thrive.

范文3 主题：科技双刃剑（人人低头看手机）

As is vividly portrayed in the picture, a group of friends sit around a dinner table, yet nobody talks to each other—everyone is staring at his or her smartphone. Though physically together, they are psychologically far apart. The picture reveals the double-edged nature of modern technology: it connects the distant, yet estranges the near.

It is undeniable that smartphones have brought unprecedented convenience to our lives: we can communicate across continents with a single tap. However, the overuse of such devices is quietly eroding face-to-face communication. According to a recent survey, a considerable proportion of young people admit that they spend more time chatting online than talking with their families. Consequently, the quality of interpersonal relationships is declining, and phenomena such as "phubbing"（低头族）have triggered wide public concern.

In my view, technology itself is neutral; what matters is how we use it. Therefore, it is advisable for us to put down our phones at dinner tables and spend more quality time with the people around us. Only by using technology wisely can we enjoy its benefits without sacrificing genuine human connection.

范文4 主题：诚信（商家以次充好 vs 诚信经营）

As is ironically depicted in the picture, a merchant is replacing the label "premium quality" with "inferior goods" on a box of products, while customers, unaware of the trick, are queuing to buy. The picture exposes a disturbing phenomenon: in pursuit of short-term profits, some businesses have abandoned the most basic principle of integrity.

Dishonest business practices, such as selling fake or shoddy products, may bring immediate gains, but they are self-destructive in the long run. Once consumers lose trust, no advertisement can win them back. A case in point is that several once-famous brands collapsed overnight after scandals of counterfeiting were exposed. By contrast, enterprises that value honesty accumulate reputation, which is precisely the most precious intangible asset in the market economy.

From my perspective, integrity is to business what credibility is to a person. Therefore, supervision should be strengthened so that dishonest merchants pay a heavy price, and consumers ought to boycott dishonest products consciously. Only when integrity becomes the bottom line of every company can the market enjoy healthy and sustainable development.

范文5 主题：环保（绿水青山）

As is vividly illustrated in the picture, the same river is presented in two contrasting scenes: in the past, it was so polluted that fish floated belly-up, whereas now, after years of treatment, children are playing happily along its clear banks. The picture conveys a profound message: lucid waters and lush green mountains are invaluable assets.

Environmental degradation is, in essence, the price of ignoring nature's limits. For decades, some regions sacrificed the environment for short-term economic gains, only to spend even more money repairing the damage. This teaches us that economic development and environmental protection are not contradictory but complementary: a beautiful environment itself attracts talent, tourism and investment.

From what has been discussed above, it is high time that we consolidated the achievements of environmental governance. The government should keep improving relevant laws, enterprises must adopt cleaner technologies, and every individual can contribute by living a low-carbon life. Only when green development becomes everyone's habit can we hand over clear waters and blue skies to future generations.

小作文（应用文，约100词）

范文6 建议信（向图书馆建议）

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am a senior student majoring in computer science, and I am writing to put forward some practical suggestions regarding the library service.

To begin with, it would be advisable to extend the opening hours during the examination weeks, when seats are in great demand. In addition, an online reservation system would go a long way toward solving the problem of occupied-but-empty seats. Finally, more outlets and quiet zones would make the library friendlier to those who bring laptops.

I hope you will find these suggestions constructive. Looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely, Li Ming

范文7 邀请信（邀请教授做讲座）

Dear Professor Wang,

On behalf of the Graduate Student Union, I am honored to invite you to deliver a lecture on "Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Education" for our postgraduate forum.

The lecture is scheduled for 2:00 p.m. on Friday, December 10th, in Lecture Hall 3 of the main building, and will last about 90 minutes, followed by a question-and-answer session. It would be our great honor if you could accept this invitation, as your insights are highly expected by all the attendees.

Please feel free to contact me at liming@university.edu.cn if you have any questions. Looking forward to your favorable reply.

Yours sincerely, Li Ming

范文8 感谢信（感谢外教）

Dear Ms. Smith,

I am writing to extend my heartfelt gratitude for your patient guidance during my preparation for the speech contest.

But for your help, I could not have overcome my fear of public speaking. You not only corrected my pronunciation word by word, but also taught me how to organize ideas logically and connect with the audience. Thanks to your encouragement, I eventually won the first prize, which I had never dreamed of.

My appreciation is beyond words. I sincerely hope to have another chance to learn from you, and I will pass on what you taught me to my fellow students.

Yours sincerely, Li Ming

范文9 通知（招募志愿者）

Notice

Volunteers are needed for the International Culture Festival, which is to be held on the Central Plaza of our university from June 15th to June 17th. The volunteers are expected to receive foreign guests, guide campus tours and assist in on-site coordination.

Applicants are required to be fluent in spoken English, familiar with our campus, and available throughout the festival. Those interested please sign up at the Student Union Office or send your resume to union@university.edu.cn before June 1st.

The Student Union May 20th

范文10 道歉信（失约道歉）

Dear Zhang Wei,

I am terribly sorry for failing to show up at our appointment yesterday afternoon, and please accept my sincere apology.

The reason for my absence was that my grandmother was suddenly taken to hospital, and I rushed there without my phone at hand. I fully understand your disappointment, as we had made the plan a week in advance.

To make up for it, I would like to invite you to dinner this Saturday, and I have already prepared a small gift for you. I assure you that such a thing will never happen again.

Yours sincerely, Li Ming
