英语 · 长难句精析 170 句（扩展版 · 2026-10-29续增100句）

《长难句精析30句》的进阶续篇：170句按十七个结构专题分类（A–Q，2026-10-29由70句扩至170句），每句给【拆】（结构标注）+【译】（参考译文）。所有句子为自编考研风格句（覆盖政经科技社会题材）。 用法：先自己划主干再看【拆】；每天10句，一周过完；二刷时遮住译文做"视译"。 配套：《语法_知识点与难点》《长难句精析30句》。

A. 后置定语与同位语（10句）

  1. The ability to communicate complex ideas clearly — a skill that many technical specialists lack — has become the single most valuable asset in the modern workplace. 【拆】主干：The ability has become the asset。to communicate…clearly 不定式后置定语；破折号内为同位语（a skill）+ that 定语从句。 【译】清晰传达复杂思想的能力——许多技术专家缺乏的一种技能——已成为现代职场最有价值的资产。

  2. Evidence from a decade of trials in developing countries suggests that cash transfers, long dismissed as charity, can improve school attendance more effectively than some formal programs. 【拆】主干：Evidence suggests that…。from a decade…后置定语；long dismissed as charity 为过去分词插入（修饰 cash transfers）。 【译】发展中国家十年试验的证据表明，长期被视为慈善施舍的现金补贴，在提高入学率方面比某些正式项目更有效。

  3. The assumption that machines will simply replace workers ignores the possibility that new categories of employment may emerge. 【拆】两个 that 引导同位语从句（分别解释 assumption 与 possibility）。 【译】"机器将简单取代工人"这一假设忽视了新就业类别可能出现这一可能。

  4. Companies operating in markets where consumers expect instant replies are shifting customer service from humans to systems that never sleep. 【拆】operating…现在分词后置定语；where 定语从句修饰 markets；that never sleep 修饰 systems。 【译】身处消费者期待即时回复的市场，各公司正把客服从人工转向永不休息的系统。

  5. One reason for the persistence of the myth, historians argue, lies in the nineteenth-century textbooks that first told the story. 【拆】historians argue 插入语；for the persistence of the myth 后置定语；that 定语从句。 【译】历史学家认为，这一迷思之所以经久不衰，原因之一在于最早讲述这段故事的19世纪教科书。

  6. The proposal, a compromise between unions and management, would link pay rises to productivity rather than to inflation. 【拆】a compromise…为 proposal 的同位语；rather than 对比结构。 【译】该提案是工会与管理层之间的折中方案，将把加薪与生产率而非通胀挂钩。

  7. Anybody who has watched a toddler destroy a living room understands that curiosity is not a skill to be taught but an impulse to be guided. 【拆】who 定语从句；that 宾语从句内含 not A but B。 【译】任何见过幼儿"扫荡"客厅的人都明白：好奇心不是一门可教的技能，而是一种需要引导的冲动。

  8. Researchers studying the sleep patterns of teenagers found that early school start times may conflict with biological rhythms that shift during adolescence. 【拆】studying 分词定语；that 宾从；that shift…修饰 rhythms。 【译】研究青少年睡眠模式的学者发现，过早到校上课可能与青春期发生偏移的生物节律相冲突。

  9. The report's central claim — that reading on paper produces deeper comprehension than reading on screens — remains contested. 【拆】破折号内 that 同位语从句说明 claim 的内容。 【译】该报告的核心主张——纸质阅读比屏幕阅读理解更深——仍有争议。

  10. A city that plans for cars alone will get congestion; a city that plans for people gets streets worth walking. 【拆】分号并列两个分句，各含 that 定语从句；worth walking 后置定语。 【译】只为汽车规划的城市得到拥堵；为人规划的城市得到值得漫步的街道。

B. 分词结构与独立主格（10句）

  1. Having spent two years collecting data, the team published findings that contradicted its own earlier hypothesis. 【拆】Having spent…完成式分词作状语（时间先后）；that 定语从句。 【译】在花两年收集数据之后，该团队发表了与自己早先假设相矛盾的发现。

  2. Stimulated by tax breaks, investment in renewable energy rose by a third, with solar accounting for most of the increase. 【拆】Stimulated…过去分词状语（被动）；with solar accounting…独立主格。 【译】在税收优惠的刺激下，可再生能源投资增长三分之一，其中太阳能占大头。

  3. Judged by conventional measures of success, the venture looked like a failure; judged by what it taught the industry, it was invaluable. 【拆】两个 Judged by…分词状语，形成对照；what 引导宾语从句。 【译】以传统成败标准衡量，这家企业像是失败；以它给行业带来的启示衡量，它价值连城。

  4. The conference ended without agreement, delegates accusing each other of negotiating in bad faith. 【拆】delegates accusing…独立主格（名词+现在分词）表伴随结果。 【译】会议无果而终，代表们互相指责对方缺乏谈判诚意。

  5. Followed by reporters, the minister declined to comment, saying only that the matter was under review. 【拆】Followed by…过去分词状语；saying only that…现在分词状语内含宾从。 【译】在记者的追逐下，部长拒绝置评，只表示事情正在审查之中。

  6. Not wanting to alarm passengers, the crew described the incident as a minor technical fault. 【拆】Not wanting…否定式现在分词状语（原因）。 【译】不想让乘客恐慌，机组把这起事件说成轻微技术故障。

  7. Much of the language in the contract being deliberately vague, disputes were almost inevitable. 【拆】"名词+being+形容词"独立主格作原因状语。 【译】合同措辞大有意含糊之处，纠纷几乎不可避免。

  8. Confronted with falling revenues, the newspaper cut its newsroom by half, a decision that critics say doomed its quality. 【拆】Confronted with…过去分词状语；a decision 同位语 + that 从句（内含 say 插入）。 【译】面对收入下滑，这家报社裁员一半，批评者称这一决定葬送了其内容质量。

  9. With winter approaching and demand rising, energy prices are expected to remain high well into next year. 【拆】with + 名词 + 分词 ×2 并列独立主格。 【译】随着冬季来临、需求上涨，能源价格预计将在明年大部分时间保持高位。

  10. Having been warned repeatedly, the company can hardly claim it did not know the risks. 【拆】Having been warned 完成式被动分词；claim 后省略 that 的宾从。 【译】屡次被警告之后，这家公司很难辩称自己不知道风险。

C. 从句嵌套（10句）

  1. What the study does not explain is why participants who received identical instructions produced such different results. 【拆】What 主语从句 + is + why 表语从句；who 定语从句嵌在 why 从句中。 【译】该研究没有解释的是：为何拿到完全相同指令的被试会产出如此不同的结果。

  2. The committee's refusal to publish the minutes — a decision justified on grounds that disclosure would inhibit frank discussion — has deepened suspicion. 【拆】to publish 不定式定语；a decision 同位语；justified 分词定语；on grounds that…同位语从句（that=原因内容）。 【译】委员会拒绝公布会议纪要——其理由是公开会妨碍畅所欲言——这加深了公众的怀疑。

  3. Whether a four-day week improves productivity depends less on hours than on how meetings and interruptions are handled. 【拆】Whether 主语从句；less…than…比较；how 引导介词宾语从句。 【译】四天工作制能否提升效率，关键不在时长，而在会议与干扰如何处理。

  4. It is precisely because the technology works so well in the lab that its failure in the field surprises so many engineers. 【拆】It is…that 强调句强调 because 原因从句。 【译】正因为这项技术在实验室表现太好，它在现场"失灵"才让这么多工程师意外。

  5. There is a case for saying that the era in which a single newspaper could shape national opinion has passed. 【拆】saying that…介宾从句；in which 定语从句（era）。 【译】有理由认为，一家报纸就能左右全国舆论的时代已经过去。

  6. The question is not whether automation will displace some tasks — it already has — but whether the gains will be shared. 【拆】not whether…but whether 并列表语；破折号内 it already has 为省略式插入（has displaced）。 【译】问题不是自动化是否会取代某些工作——它已经在取代——而是其收益能否被分享。

  7. Economists who assumed that consumers would respond rationally to price signals have had to confront evidence that they often do not. 【拆】who 定语从句 + that 宾从（assume）；evidence 后 that 同位语从句；两个 they 都指 consumers。 【译】那些假定消费者会对价格信号理性反应的经济学家，不得不面对消费者常常不理性的证据。

  8. Few graduates leave university knowing that the skills employers prize most are learned, if at all, on the job. 【拆】knowing 现在分词伴随状语内含 that 宾从；if at all 插入（="如果真要学的话"）。 【译】几乎没有毕业生在离校时就知道：雇主最看重的技能——如果真能学到——是在工作岗位上习得的。

  9. The proposal that all articles funded by public money should be freely available has divided researchers, some of whom worry that journals will pass costs back to authors. 【拆】that 同位语从句；some of whom 非限定定语从句；worry that 宾从。 【译】"公共资金资助的论文应免费开放"的提议使研究者分裂，其中一些人担心期刊会把成本转嫁给作者。

  10. Where once a degree guaranteed a stable career, employers now routinely weigh what candidates can do over where they studied. 【拆】Where 状语从句（对比今昔）；weigh A over B 结构；what/where 宾语从句。 【译】过去一纸文凭就能保障稳定职业，如今雇主更看重候选人能做什么，而非毕业院校。

D. 倒装与强调（10句）

  1. Not until regulators began fining platforms for data breaches did firms treat privacy as a design priority. 【拆】Not until…前置引发部分倒装（did firms treat）。 【译】直到监管机构开始就数据泄露处罚平台，企业才把隐私当作设计上的头等大事。

  2. Rarely has a technology been adopted so quickly by so many people with so little public debate. 【拆】Rarely 否定副词开头 → has a technology been adopted 倒装。 【译】鲜有一项技术在没有多少公共讨论的情况下，被如此多的人如此迅速地采用。

  3. Only when the last factory in the region closed did officials admit that retraining programs had failed. 【拆】Only when…状语前置 → did officials admit 倒装。 【译】直到该地区最后一家工厂关闭，官员们才承认再就业培训计划失败了。

  4. So uncertain are the long-term effects of the drug that some doctors refuse to prescribe it to new patients. 【拆】So + adj. 前置 → are the long-term effects 倒装；that 结果从句。 【译】该药长期效果如此不确定，以至于一些医生拒绝给新病人开这种药。

  5. Among the participants were farmers who had traveled two days to attend the hearing. 【拆】表语 Among…前置全倒装（were farmers）。 【译】与会者中有奔波两天前来参加听证会的农民。

  6. It was the trial's unexpected outcome, rather than its size, that made headlines around the world. 【拆】It was…that 强调句，强调主语；rather than 插入对比。 【译】让这项试验登上全球头条的，是其出乎意料的结果，而非其规模。

  7. Hardly had the system gone live when complaints began to arrive. 【拆】Hardly…when…（一…就…）倒装结构。 【译】系统刚上线，投诉就开始涌来。

  8. Should the migration continue at this pace, the region's labor force will shrink by a third within a decade. 【拆】Should 倒装= If the migration should continue（虚拟条件）。 【译】如果人口外流以此速度持续，该地区劳动力十年内将缩减三分之一。

  9. Not only does the scheme reduce emissions; it also lowers household bills, a combination rare in environmental policy. 【拆】Not only 句首倒装；a combination 同位语 + rare 后置定语。 【译】该计划不仅减排，还降低家庭账单——这种兼得在环境政策中实属罕见。

  10. Little did the founders imagine that their garage project would one day employ tens of thousands. 【拆】Little 否定词前置 → did the founders imagine 倒装。 【译】创始人当年做梦也想不到，车库里的项目有一天会雇用数万人。

E. 省略与替代（10句）

  1. When asked about the delay, the spokesman referred questions to the contractor. 【拆】When (he was) asked 状语从句省略。 【译】被问及延期时，发言人把问题推给了承包商。

  2. Though frequently criticized, the practice remains standard across the industry. 【拆】Though (it is) frequently criticized。 【译】尽管屡遭批评，这一做法仍是全行业标准。

  3. The first solution failed; the second, if less elegant, proved cheaper. 【拆】if (it was) less elegant 插入省略。 【译】第一种方案失败了；第二种虽不优雅，却更省钱。

  4. Errors, whether caused by software or by fatigue, must be reported within 24 hours. 【拆】whether (they are) caused 插入省略。 【译】错误——无论由软件还是疲劳造成——必须在24小时内上报。

  5. If confirmed, the finding would overturn two decades of assumptions. 【拆】If (it is) confirmed。 【译】如果得到证实，这一发现将推翻二十年来的假设。

  6. She has read more papers than anyone else in the lab. 【拆】比较从句省略 has read。 【译】她读过的论文比实验室任何人都多。

  7. While admitting the mistake, the editor refused to resign. 【拆】While (he was) admitting。 【译】主编承认错误，但拒绝辞职。

  8. Some firms have automated customer support; others, for now, have chosen not to. 【拆】not to = not to have automated（替代省略）。 【译】一些公司已把客服自动化；另一些公司暂时选择不那么做。

  9. The scheme, once universal, is now available only to veterans. 【拆】once (it was) universal 插入省略。 【译】该计划曾经人人可享，如今只面向退伍军人。

  10. Where possible, patients are treated at home rather than in hospital. 【拆】Where (it is) possible。 【译】条件允许时，病人在家接受治疗而非住院。

F. 插入语与破折号（10句）

  1. The reforms, economists generally agree, came too late to prevent the crisis. 【拆】economists generally agree 全句插入。 【译】经济学家普遍认为，改革来得太晚，未能阻止危机。

  2. Local courts — some of them barely staffed — have struggled to process the surge in cases. 【拆】破折号内独立主格式插入（some of them barely staffed）。 【译】地方法院——其中一些几乎无人可用——正艰难应对案件激增。

  3. The company's founder, once dismissed as a dreamer, now advises governments on technology policy. 【拆】once dismissed as…过去分词插入。 【译】这位公司创始人曾被讥为空想家，如今为各国政府担任技术政策顾问。

  4. Cheaper sensors, it turns out, change not only what engineers can measure but what they choose to measure. 【拆】it turns out 插入；not only…but…连接两个 what 从句。 【译】事实证明，更便宜的传感器不仅改变了工程师能测什么，也改变了他们选择测什么。

  5. The drug's benefits, though real, are smaller than early trials suggested, researchers now concede. 【拆】though real 插入省略；researchers now concede 句尾插入。 【译】研究者现在承认，该药的益处虽然真实，却比早期试验显示的要小。

  6. Immigrants — legal and undocumented alike — make up a fifth of the region's workforce. 【拆】破折号内形容词并列插入。 【译】移民——无论合法还是无证——占该地区劳动力的五分之一。

  7. What matters, in the end, is not the tool but the questions users bring to it. 【拆】in the end 插入；what 主语从句；users bring to it 定语从句（省略that）。 【译】归根结底，重要的不是工具，而是使用者带给工具的问题。

  8. The verdict, whatever its legal merits, will almost certainly be appealed. 【拆】whatever 让步插入。 【译】无论该裁决在法律上有何道理，几乎肯定会被上诉。

  9. Big banks, unlike their smaller rivals, can absorb the cost of compliance. 【拆】unlike…介词短语插入对比。 【译】大银行不像小对手那样，它们能消化合规成本。

  10. The findings, if they hold up, point to a cheap intervention with outsized effects. 【拆】if they hold up 插入条件。 【译】如果这些发现站得住脚，它们指向一种成本低廉但效果巨大的干预手段。

G. 虚拟、比较与否定转移（10句）

  1. Had the bank acted on the warnings, the losses might have been far smaller. 【拆】Had…= If the bank had acted（虚拟过去倒装）。 【译】如果银行当初理会那些警告，损失本可能小得多。

  2. It is essential that every experiment be documented before the results are announced. 【拆】essential 后接虚拟语气（should）be documented。 【译】至关重要的一点是：在结果公布前，每项实验都必须留有记录。

  3. Without the subsidy, few companies would have bothered to retrofit old buildings. 【拆】Without 介词短语=虚拟条件；would have bothered 虚拟过去。 【译】没有补贴，几乎没有公司会费力改造旧建筑。

  4. The more the platform personalizes content, the less likely users are to encounter views unlike their own. 【拆】the more…the less…双重比较；likely 句型：sb. is likely to do。 【译】平台内容越个性化，用户遇到异己观点的可能性越小。

  5. Nothing suggests the trend will reverse; everything suggests it will accelerate. 【拆】nothing/everything 对仗；suggest（表明）后宾从。 【译】没有任何迹象表明趋势会逆转；一切迹象都表明它将加速。

  6. Scientists caution against concluding that correlation, however consistent, proves causation. 【拆】caution against + 动名词；that 同位语从句；however consistent 插入让步。 【译】科学家告诫：不要因为相关性（无论多么稳定一致）就断定因果关系。

  7. I would rather the committee published the raw data than relied on a summary. 【拆】would rather + 从句用过去式表虚拟。 【译】我宁愿委员会公布原始数据，也不要依赖一份摘要。

  8. The remedy proved no more effective than doing nothing at all. 【拆】no more…than…（和…一样不…）特殊比较。 【译】事实证明，这种补救措施与什么都不做同样无效。

  9. Contrary to what most people assume, multitasking does not save time. 【拆】Contrary to + what 从句。 【译】与多数人的想法相反，多任务处理并不省时间。

  10. It never occurred to the designers that users might prefer simplicity to power. 【拆】It occurred to sb. that…句型的否定；prefer A to B。 【译】设计者从未想到：用户可能宁要简单，不要强大。

H. 分裂结构：主谓被长定语隔开（10句）

  1. The belief that a single test can capture the full range of human ability has survived largely because it simplifies administration. 【拆】主干：The belief has survived。that 同位语从句把主语和谓语远远隔开。 【译】"一次考试就能测出人的全部能力"这一信念之所以长存，主要因为它简化了管理工作。

  2. Evidence gathered over four decades from more than thirty countries points in the same direction. 【拆】主干：Evidence points in the same direction。gathered…过去分词后置定语极长，主谓被分裂。 【译】四十多年来从三十多个国家收集的证据，指向同一个方向。

  3. The promise that automation would give workers more leisure was made by almost every generation of inventors since the loom. 【拆】主干：The promise was made。that 同位语从句插入中间；since the loom="自织布机以来"。 【译】"自动化将给工人带来更多闲暇"——自织布机时代以来，几乎每一代发明家都做过这个承诺。

  4. Reports that the committee had falsified data circulated for weeks before anyone asked for the originals. 【拆】主干：Reports circulated。that 同位语从句；注意别把 reports 误当动词。 【译】"委员会伪造数据"的传闻流传了数周，才有人索要原始材料。

  5. The instinct to protect children from every possible harm, however understandable, deprives them of the practice they will need as adults. 【拆】主干：The instinct deprives them of…。to protect…不定式定语+however 插入+省略 that 的定语从句。 【译】让孩子免受一切伤害的本能虽可理解，却剥夺了他们成年后必需的练习。

  6. A claim made in confidence is not the same as a claim that survives confidence intervals. 【拆】主干：A claim is not the same as a claim。made in confidence 分词定语；第二个 that 从句修饰第二个 claim。 【译】口头上有把握的说法，不等于经得起置信区间检验的说法。

  7. The suggestion that employees work four days for full pay has moved from heresy to pilot program within a decade. 【拆】主干：The suggestion has moved。that 同位语从句；from A to B 介词结构作状语。 【译】"员工全薪上四天班"的建议，十年之内已从异端邪说变成了试点项目。

  8. Data collected on people who never consented to collection is data collected on all of us. 【拆】主干：Data is data。三个分词短语层层修饰，冷峻排比。 【译】在从未同意被收集的人身上收集的数据，就是在我们所有人身上收集的数据。

  9. The habit of reading the conclusion before the method, natural as it seems, is how a field quietly loses its way. 【拆】主干：The habit is how…。of reading…介词定语；natural as it seems 让步插入；how 表语从句。 【译】先看结论再看方法——这个看似自然的习惯，正是一个学科悄悄走偏的方式。

  10. Legislation intended to close one loophole has a way of opening two, usually in places no one thought to look. 【拆】主干：Legislation has a way of doing。intended to…分词定语；usually in places…状语+省略that定语从句。 【译】意在堵住一个漏洞的立法，往往顺手打开两个——通常开在没人想到要看的角落。

I. 多重否定与半否定（10句）

  1. It is not that the evidence is absent; it is that no one has been paid to look for it. 【拆】not that A; it is that B（"不是……而是……"平行表语从句）。 【译】并非证据不存在，而是没人领着薪水去找它。

  2. Nothing in the rulebook prevents the practice; what prevents it is the risk of being caught in the act of explaining it. 【拆】Nothing prevents A; what prevents it is B（强调句式化用）；in the act of="正在……时"。 【译】规则手册里没有任何条款阻止这种做法；阻止它的是"解释它时被抓个正着"的风险。

  3. Few findings are as inconvenient as those that arrive too late to change anything but early enough to embarrass everyone. 【拆】Few…as…as（半否定比较）；too…to…（"太……而不能"）与 early enough to（"恰好足以"）对照。 【译】很少有发现像"来得太迟、改变不了什么，却来得够早、足以让所有人难堪"的发现那样令人不便。

  4. Hardly anyone denies that the system needs reform; almost everyone disagrees on which part to sacrifice first. 【拆】Hardly anyone（半否定="几乎没人"）；almost everyone（对照）。 【译】几乎没有人否认这个系统需要改革；也几乎人人都不同意先牺牲哪一部分。

  5. Not until the third audit did anyone notice that the numbers had been copied, errors included, from the previous year. 【拆】Not until 倒装；errors included 独立主格插入。 【译】直到第三次审计才有人注意到：那些数字连同错误一起，是从上一年原样抄来的。

  6. The company never said the product was safe; it said only that no evidence of harm had been found — a distinction that lawyers value and customers do not. 【拆】never said A; said only that B（两次否定程度对比）；a distinction that…同位语+定从。 【译】公司从没说产品安全，它只说"尚未发现危害的证据"——律师们珍视这个区别，顾客们不。

  7. Seldom has a technology been adopted so quickly with so little thought given to who would bear its costs. 【拆】Seldom has…been（半否定倒装）；with so little thought given to…（独立主格式介宾）。 【译】很少有哪项技术被采用得这么快，而对"谁来承担代价"想得这么少。

  8. What cannot be said directly is said in footnotes, and footnotes, in this field, are where the real arguments live. 【拆】What…is said in…（主语从句被动）；where 表语从句。 【译】不能直说的话被写进脚注，而在这个领域，真正的争论恰恰栖身于脚注。

  9. Barely a month passes without a new model claiming to have solved, once and for all, a problem that has resisted solution for decades. 【拆】Barely…without…（双重否定="几乎每个月都"）；once and for all="一劳永逸地"。 【译】几乎每个月都有新模型宣称一劳永逸地解决了一个几十年来始终无解的问题。

  10. It would be wrong to say the guidelines were ignored; rather, they were read so carefully that their meaning evaporated. 【拆】It would be wrong to say（委婉否定）；rather 转折；so…that 结果从句。 【译】说这些指导方针被无视是不对的；不如说，它们被研读得太仔细，以至于含义都蒸发了。

J. 三重以上平行结构（10句）

  1. The successful applicant will be able to write clearly, argue precisely, and admit error publicly — three abilities that no entrance exam reliably measures. 【拆】三个 to do 平行；破折号后同位语+定语从句。 【译】成功的申请者要能写得清楚、辩得精准、并公开认错——入学考试无法可靠测量的三种能力。

  2. What the reform promised was cheaper care, faster diagnosis, and kinder treatment, delivered all at once, which is precisely why it failed. 【拆】What 主语从句；三个比较级形容词平行；delivered all at once 分词；which 指代整个承诺。 【译】改革承诺的是更便宜的医疗、更快的诊断、更温和的对待，而且要同时兑现——它之所以失败，恰恰因为这个。

  3. The book has been burned, banned, misquoted, and finally assigned — the standard biography of a dangerous idea. 【拆】四个过去分词谓语平行；破折号同位语总结。 【译】这本书被焚毁过、被查禁过、被断章取义过，最终被列入必读书目——一个危险思想的标配一生。

  4. To govern is to choose; to choose is to disappoint someone; and the art lies not in avoiding disappointment but in deserving the disappointment one causes. 【拆】三个不定式判断句排比；not in A but in B；deserve="配得上/当之无愧"。 【译】治理即选择；选择即让某些人失望；而艺术不在于避免失望，在于让自己的失望"给得名副其实"。

  5. Founded by idealists, funded by pragmatists, and eventually run by neither, the institution spent its second century discovering what it had been for. 【拆】三个过去分词开头平行（by 介宾各配一类人）；neither="两拨人都不是"。 【译】由理想主义者创立、由实用主义者出资、最终由两者之外的什么人运营——这家机构用第二个一百年搞清了自己究竟是干什么的。

  6. Children learn language without grammar books, adults learn cities without maps, and everyone learns grief without instructions. 【拆】三句平行，各含"无X而学Y"结构；grief="哀伤"。 【译】孩子没有语法书也学会语言，成年人没有地图也学会城市，而哀伤，人人都是在没有说明书的情况下学会的。

  7. The report catalogs what we lack, what we pretend to have, and what we have stopped wanting — and the third list is the longest. 【拆】三个 what 从句平行作 catalogs 的宾语；破折号后转折点睛。 【译】报告清点了我们缺什么、我们假装有什么、以及我们已经不再想要什么——而第三张清单最长。

  8. Great teachers correct less, ask more, and wait longer than the syllabus allows. 【拆】三个比较级动词短语平行；than the syllabus allows 比较从句。 【译】优秀的老师比教学大纲允许的更少纠正、更多提问、等待得更久。

  9. The field has produced more synonyms than solutions, more conferences than cures, and more retiring keynote speakers than retired hypotheses. 【拆】三组 more A than B 平行；retiring/retired 双关。 【译】这个领域产出的同义词多于解决方案，会议多于疗法，退休返聘做主旨演讲的学者多于真正退休的假说。

  10. What survives of any life, in the end, is what was given away: the taught lesson, the kept promise, the answered call at three in the morning. 【拆】What survives 主语从句；what was given away 表语从句；冒号后三个名词短语平行（分词定语各配一件小事）。 【译】一生到最后留下来的，是那些给出去的东西：教过的一课、守住的一诺、凌晨三点接起的一通电话。

K. 让步与条件的暗含表达（10句）

  1. However careful the sampling, a survey can only tell you about the people who answer surveys. 【拆】However + adj 提前构成让步；第二个分支暗含限定。 【译】抽样再谨慎，一份问卷也只能告诉你"愿意答问卷的人"长什么样。

  2. Grant that the method is sound; the conclusion still outruns the data by several paragraphs. 【拆】Grant that…（祈使式让步="姑且承认"）；still 转折；outruns="超出了"。 【译】就算方法无懈可击，结论仍然比数据多跑出了好几段。

  3. Were the funding doubled tomorrow, the bottleneck would merely move from money to time. 【拆】Were 虚拟倒装（=If the funding were doubled）。 【译】即便经费明天就翻倍，瓶颈也只会从钱移到时间上。

  4. For all its talk of disruption, the industry has changed remarkably little about how power is exercised. 【拆】For all…（介词让步="尽管"）；remarkably little（"几乎没变"）。 【译】尽管满口颠覆创新，这个行业在权力如何行使这件事上几乎纹丝未动。

  5. Admit the premise, and the conclusion follows whether you like it or not. 【拆】祈使句+and="只要……就"；whether or not 让步。 【译】承认了前提，结论就会跟着来——不管你喜不喜欢。

  6. The finding holds even granting every limitation the authors themselves list. 【拆】even granting…（让步分词）；the authors themselves list 省略 that 定语从句。 【译】即便把作者自己列出的所有限制条件都算上，这个发现依然成立。

  7. Only if the two groups were truly comparable would the comparison mean what it appears to mean. 【拆】Only if 条件+倒装 would the comparison mean；mean what 表语从句。 【译】只有当两组人真正可比时，这个比较才配得上它表面上想要表达的意思。

  8. Failing that, the fallback is not fewer regulations but better ones, which is harder, not easier. 【拆】Failing that 分词="若此路不通"；not A but B；harder, not easier 补充对比。 【译】若此路不通，退路不是更少的监管，而是更好的监管——这更难，而不是更简单。

  9. Whether or not one accepts the theory, its vocabulary has colonized the conversation. 【拆】Whether or not 让步；colonize 隐喻="殖民了整个话语"。 【译】无论你是否接受这套理论，它的词汇已经殖民了整场对话。

  10. Much as the managers would prefer otherwise, the deadline is set by physics, not by committees. 【拆】Much as…（让步倒装="尽管非常想"）；not by A 结构收尾。 【译】尽管经理们巴不得不是这样，工期是由物理规律定的，不是委员会定的。

L. 比较结构的高级陷阱（10句）

  1. The committee accomplished no more than a delay, though a delay, its chair noted, is sometimes all a crisis allows. 【拆】no more than="仅仅"；though 让步插入；all a crisis allows="危机所能允许的全部"。 【译】委员会达成的不过是拖延一场；不过主席指出，有时候危机本身也只容得下一场拖延。

  2. It is one thing to predict the average and quite another to predict the year, which is why insurers sleep well and economists do not. 【拆】one thing…quite another；which 指代整句；sleep well 幽默对照。 【译】预测平均值是一回事，预测哪一年是另一回事——这正是保险睡得香而经济学家睡不着的原因。

  3. Nothing is cheaper than certainty purchased after the results are known. 【拆】Nothing…than 最高级否定；purchased after…分词定语。 【译】没有比"结果出来之后再买的确定感"更便宜的东西了。

  4. More is known about the surface of Mars than about the floor of our own ocean, and the gap is not closing. 【拆】more…than 比较的宾语是"知识"；and the gap 补充。 【译】我们对火星表面的了解，多于对自己海底的了解——而这个差距并没有在缩小。

  5. The sooner a society admits the trade-off exists, the less it costs to make it honestly. 【拆】the sooner…the less…平行比较级；make it honestly="诚实地做出取舍"。 【译】一个社会越早承认取舍的存在，诚实做出取舍的代价就越小。

  6. Not so much a theory as a mood, the paper nonetheless changed what departments were willing to fund. 【拆】Not so much A as B 前置（"与其说是理论，不如说是一种情绪"）；nonetheless 转折。 【译】这篇论文与其说是一种理论，不如说是一种情绪；尽管如此，它改变了各院系愿意资助什么。

  7. Twice as many students completed the course as had enrolled, a result that tells you something about record-keeping. 【拆】twice as many…as…（两倍于入学人数完成了课程=荒谬）；a result that…同位语+定从（讽刺）。 【译】完成课程的学生是入学人数的两倍——这个结果告诉你的是他们的登记造册水平如何。

  8. Better, the authors argue, to admit uncertainty loudly than to discover it quietly in year five. 【拆】Better to A than to B 的倒装省略；loudly/quietly 对仗。 【译】作者认为，与其第五年才悄悄发现不确定，不如一开始就大声承认它。

  9. No sooner had the policy been announced than the exemptions began to multiply. 【拆】No sooner…than…倒装固定搭配（"刚……就……"）。 【译】政策刚一宣布，豁免条款就开始成倍繁殖。

  10. As much art as science, the allocation of attention decides which problems a civilization is permitted to notice. 【拆】As much A as B 前置同位（"既是艺术也是科学"）；be permitted to notice="获准注意到"。 【译】注意力的分配既是艺术也是科学——它决定一个文明被允许注意到哪些问题。

M. 动名词与不定式的分工（10句）

  1. Admitting a mistake is an event; learning from it is a habit, and the two are separated by an ego. 【拆】动名词作主语两连；separated by an ego="被自尊心隔开"（点睛）。 【译】承认错误是一个事件，从错误中学习是一种习惯——两者之间隔着一个自我。

  2. To keep asking "why" is the cheapest form of quality control available to any institution. 【拆】To keep asking 不定式主语；available to 后置定语。 【译】坚持追问"为什么"，是任何机构都负担得起的最便宜的质量控制。

  3. There is no shame in changing one's mind; the shame belongs to the years spent defending the change that never came. 【拆】no shame in doing；spent defending…过去分词定语+动名词；the change that never came（定从）。 【译】改变想法并不丢人；丢人的是那些用来捍卫"永远不会发生的改变"的年月。

  4. Writing clearly requires deciding clearly, and deciding clearly requires accepting that some readers will disagree. 【拆】两个动名词主语连环；accepting that…动名词带宾从。 【译】写清楚的前提是想清楚，而想清楚的前提是接受"总会有读者不同意"。

  5. The temptation to keep every option open is, in the end, the temptation never to have lived. 【拆】to keep…不定式定语；in the end 插入；never to have lived 完成式不定式。 【译】把所有选项都保持敞开的诱惑，说到底，是"从不真正活过"的诱惑。

  6. Having opinions is easy; holding them after the evidence arrives is the expensive part. 【拆】Having vs holding 两个动名词对照；after the evidence arrives 时间从句。 【译】有观点很容易；在证据抵达之后还持有观点，才是费钱的部分。

  7. It took the field twenty years to admit that the elegant solution did not work, and five more to ask why anyone had expected it to. 【拆】It took sb. time to do；admit that 宾从；expect it to（省略 work="期望它起作用"）。 【译】这个学科花了二十年才承认那个漂亮的理论不管用，又花了五年才追问：当初凭什么指望它管用？

  8. Reading without note-taking is tourism; note-taking without rereading is hoarding. 【拆】两个动名词比喻句对仗；tourism vs hoarding。 【译】只读不记是观光，只记不重读是囤积。

  9. Saying "the data will decide" is itself a decision — about what counts as data. 【拆】Saying…动名词主语；破折号后补充说明（about 介宾）。 【译】说一句"让数据说话"，本身就是一种决定——决定什么才有资格算作数据。

  10. To apologize promptly is to economize on everything else. 【拆】To A is to B 判断句；economize on="节省"。 【译】及时道歉，就是在其他一切事情上省钱。

N. 长主语：从句与动名词作主语（10句）

  1. That the software is free does not mean that maintaining it is, and the second free is the expensive one. 【拆】That 主语从句；mean that 从句中省略表语 free；第二个 free 一语双关。 【译】软件免费不意味着维护它免费——真正烧钱的是第二个"免费"。

  2. Whether any of this can be scaled beyond the laboratory is precisely the question the funding agencies keep deferring. 【拆】Whether 主语从句；precisely the question…同位+省略that定从；defer="拖延"。 【译】这一切能否走出实验室规模化，恰恰是资助机构一拖再拖的那个问题。

  3. What looks like patience in a teacher is, more often than not, preparation you never saw. 【拆】What 主语从句；more often than not="多半"；you never saw 省略that定从。 【译】老师身上看似的耐心，多半是你没看见的备课。

  4. How a society treats its least profitable members reveals more than any anthem it sings. 【拆】How 主语从句；reveals more than…比较；it sings 省略that定从。 【译】一个社会如何对待它最不赚钱的成员，比它唱的任何国歌都更能说明问题。

  5. That experts disagree is not a scandal; that they disagree about the same evidence is. 【拆】两个 That 主语从句；第二个省略表语 a scandal（否定转移省略）。 【译】专家有分歧不是丑闻；专家对着同一份证据有分歧，才是。

  6. Choosing which failures to publicize is, in practice, choosing what your successors will be able to see. 【拆】Choosing which…动名词主语带宾从；in practice 插入；choosing what…并列。 【译】选择公开哪些失败，实际上就是在选择你的继任者能看到什么。

  7. What the files show, in the end, is less who erred than how rarely anyone was positioned to notice. 【拆】What 主语从句；less A than B 结构；positioned to notice="处在能注意到的位置"。 【译】档案最终揭示的，与其说是谁犯了错，不如说是"几乎从来没有人处在能发现错误的位置上"。

  8. That the bridge stood is evidence of engineering; that it stood for a century is evidence of luck, and honest engineers know the difference. 【拆】两个 That 主语从句平行；and 后点睛分句。 【译】桥立住了，是工程的证据；桥立住了一百年，是运气的证据——诚实的工程师分得清这两者。

  9. Why the first draft is always bad is a question with a boring answer: thinking is hard. 【拆】Why 主语从句；a question with…介宾定语；冒号后作 answer 同位。 【译】为什么初稿总是很烂？这个问题有个无聊的答案：思考本来就难。

  10. What is truly scarce is not information but the sixty seconds of silence needed to decide whether it matters. 【拆】What 主语从句；not A but B；needed to decide…过去分词定语+whether 宾从。 【译】真正稀缺的不是信息，而是决定"它重不重要"所需的那六十秒安静。

O. 分词定语的超长链条（10句）

  1. A study published last week, funded by an industry group, citing unpublished data, has already been cited eighty times. 【拆】三个分词短语连续后置定语（published/funded/citing），一条链挂在 study 上。 【译】上周发表的一项研究——由行业组织资助、引用了未公开的数据——已经被引用了八十次。
  2. Anyone expecting a straight answer from a body designed to balance seventeen interests has not been paying attention. 【拆】expecting…现在分词定语；designed to…过去分词定语（修饰 body）；完成进行时。 【译】指望一个为平衡十七方利益而设计的机构给出直截了当的答复，说明这人一直没在关注。
  3. The children raised on screens that reward speed will judge every classroom against a standard no lecture can meet. 【拆】raised on screens 过去分词定语；that reward speed 定从；against a standard no lecture can meet 省略that双层。 【译】在"奖励手速"的屏幕上长大的一代，会用一场讲授永远无法企及的标准去评判每一间教室。
  4. Funds raised for the archive were spent, with the best intentions, on the building that houses the archive. 【拆】raised for 过去分词定语；with the best intentions 插入；that houses…定从。 【译】为档案馆筹的款，怀着最好的初衷，被花在了容纳这座档案馆的大楼上。
  5. Employees hired to automate processes have automated themselves out of three departments so far. 【拆】hired to automate 过去分词定语；automate out of="把……自动化出局"。 【译】被雇来给流程做自动化的员工，到目前为止已经把三个部门的人——包括自己——自动化出局了。
  6. Questions raised by people who will not be here in ten years are being answered by people who will. 【拆】raised by people 定语链；who will not be here 定从；by people who will（省略 be here）。 【译】由十年后不在场的人提出的问题，正在被十年后仍在场的人回答。
  7. The vocabulary invented to describe the exception has become the vocabulary used to describe the rule. 【拆】两条过去分词链；exception vs rule 对仗。 【译】为描述例外而发明的词汇，已经变成了用来描述常规的词汇。
  8. Systems built to flag abuse are, in practice, read as user manuals by those they were meant to stop. 【拆】built to flag 过去分词定语；those (that) they were meant to stop="本想拦住的人"。 【译】为标记滥用而建的系统，在实践中被滥用者当作了用户手册。
  9. Promises made in prosperity are tested only in lean years, which is why no one ever feels ready. 【拆】made in prosperity 分词定语；lean years="歉收之年"；which 指代整句。 【译】繁荣年代许下的承诺，只有歉收之年才会接受检验——这就是为什么没人觉得自己准备好了。
  10. Warnings issued after each disaster specify, with growing precision, the exact shape of the next one's negligence. 【拆】issued after…分词定语；with growing precision 插入。 【译】每场灾难之后发布的警告，以越来越高的精度，精确描画出下一场灾难的疏忽形状。
  11. Of every hundred applicants, fewer than five will read the instructions, and of those five, perhaps one will follow them. 【拆】Of every hundred…比例状语；and of those five 呼应。 【译】每一百名申请者中，读说明书的不到五个；而这五个里，大概只有一个照做。
  12. The margin of error, never larger than the assumption it rests on, is quoted with a confidence the data cannot support. 【拆】never larger than…插入定语；a confidence (that) the data cannot support 省略that定从。 【译】误差范围从来不会大于它所依赖的假设，却被引用得笃定无比——数据撑不起这份笃定。
  13. Half the variance remains unexplained, which is a polite way of saying that half the story is missing. 【拆】remains unexplained 系表被动；which 指代前句。 【译】一半的方差仍未被解释——这其实是"一半的故事还没讲"的客气说法。
  14. Three independent teams, three methods, one result: this is as close to certainty as the field has come in a generation. 【拆】名词短语排比+冒号总结；as close to A as B。 【译】三个独立团队、三种方法、一个结果：这已经是一代人之内该领域最接近确定性的时刻。
  15. Costs doubled over the decade while measured output, by the same accounting, doubled too — a coincidence that should worry auditors. 【拆】while 对比分句；by the same accounting 插入；a coincidence that…同位+定从。 【译】十年间成本翻倍，而按同一套口径统计的产出也翻倍——这种巧合应该让审计员睡不着觉。
  16. The average hides more than it reveals whenever the distribution has two humps. 【拆】hides more than it reveals；two humps="双峰分布"。 【译】只要分布是双峰的，平均值就藏着比它揭示的更多。
  17. A sample of twelve, however eloquently described, is a sample of twelve. 【拆】however 插入让步；同义反复表讽刺。 【译】十二人的样本，无论被描述得多么天花乱坠，终究是十二人的样本。
  18. The number everyone quotes is the number someone chose to compute, and the choosing is where the argument lives. 【拆】两个省略that定从；the choosing is where…表语从句。 【译】人人引用的那个数字，是有人选择去计算的那个数字；而"选择"本身，正是争论的栖身之处。
  19. Missing data is never neutral: it clusters precisely where the answer would have been most informative. 【拆】cluster="聚集"；would have been 虚拟过去。 【译】缺失的数据从不保持中立：它恰恰聚集在答案本会最有信息量的地方。
  20. Every percentage point claimed in the summary corresponds to a decision made in the appendix. 【拆】两个过去分词定语对照（summary vs appendix）。 【译】摘要里声称的每一个百分点，都对应着附录里做出的某个决定。
  21. What the committee discovered, after weeks of testimony from officials who had each approved decisions none of them remembered making, was that no one had been in charge. 【拆】What 主语从句套 after 状语套 who 定从套省略that定从；was that 表语从句。四层。 【译】委员会在数周听证后发现的——那些官员人人批准过自己都不记得做过的决定——是：从来没有人负责。
  22. The reason the lesson is taught is not that everyone fails to learn it but that the few who do learn it tend to be the ones writing the next set of rules. 【拆】is not that…but that…；the few who do learn it（do 强调）。 【译】这一课之所以要教，不是因为有太多人学不会，而是因为真正学会的那少数人，往往就是起草下一套规则的人。
  23. Had the warning been heeded when it was first issued — by, as it happens, the very committee that later claimed ignorance — the losses would have been a fraction of what they were. 【拆】Had…been heeded 虚拟倒装；the very…that 强调；a fraction of what they were。 【译】倘若警告首次发布时——碰巧正是在后来声称毫不知情的那个委员会——就被听取，损失本只会是实际损失的一个零头。
  24. That the method works is demonstrated daily by the very people whose livelihoods depend on our not understanding how it works. 【拆】That 主语从句；whose 定从套 depend on our not understanding 动名词复合结构。 【译】这套方法管用，恰恰由那些靠"我们搞不懂它怎么管用"吃饭的人每天现场演示。
  25. The bridge between what the public believes and what the evidence supports is built, rebuilt and bombed out again with such regularity that entire careers have been made on traffic alone. 【拆】between what…and what…；三个过去分词平行；such…that 结果从句。 【译】公众所信与证据所指之间的那座桥，被修起、重建、再炸毁——规律得让整个行业光靠"摆渡交通"就能立业。
  26. Anyone who has watched a funding decision made in an afternoon apply themselves to the belief that slow science is a choice rather than a luxury. 【拆】watch + 宾语 + 过去分词补语；apply themselves to；that 同位语从句。 【译】见过一笔资助在一个下午拍板的人，会一门心思相信：慢科学是一种选择，而不是奢侈品。
  27. The theory predicted, with a precision its rivals could not match, an effect so small that detecting it required instruments that did not yet exist. 【拆】with a precision (that)…插入；so small that…套 that 定从。 【译】该理论以对手无法匹敌的精度，预言了一个小到必须用"当时尚不存在的仪器"才能探测到的效应。
  28. What keeps the enterprise honest is not virtue but the certainty that, sooner or later, someone with a grudge and a spreadsheet will check. 【拆】What 主语从句；not A but B；the certainty that 同位从句。 【译】让这门事业保持诚实的不是美德，而是这样一种确定性：迟早，会有一个带着怨气与电子表格的人来查账。
  29. The generation raised on the assumption that data speaks for itself will need longer, not shorter, training in asking who paid for the microphone. 【拆】raised on 分词定语套 that 同位从句；longer, not shorter 插入强调。 【译】在"数据会自己说话"的假设中长大的一代，需要更长——而不是更短——的训练，去追问：谁为这只话筒买了单。
  30. Civilization, viewed from a sufficient distance, is the story of problems solved fast enough to reveal the next ones before anyone had learned the lessons of the last. 【拆】viewed from…分词插入；solved fast enough to…不定式结果；before anyone had learned 过去完成时从句。 【译】站在足够远处看，文明就是这样一个故事：问题被解得够快，快到在任何人学会上一课之前，就暴露出了下一课。
